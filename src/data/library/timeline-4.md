# TIMELINE IV · THE HYDRA'S CROWN

> Records from the timeline before this one. The events are on the Timeline IV page.

**THE AGENCY · COURT ALPHA**
> Court Alpha, also called the Agency, ran out of the Ebony Tower. Joining it stripped a gem of her rank, and standing came from earning the respect of the other Operatives instead. It was split into five divisions: SHADOW, the field teams; MERCY, the medics; EDGE, the engineers and smiths; INTEL; and ARCHIVE. Above them sat the Head Director and the Chancellors. Director Alina was fourth from the top.

**THE AUTHORITY · ERA 2B**
> At the coronation on Heliodor, Astral Black Diamond ended the age of Courts. Government was split into three branches. The other Diamonds' courts became corporations, and gems rose by merit, as they had in Era 1. The old currency came back, gems of Era 1 got their old riches back, and new gems could only be made from analogous pairs of essence.

**THE AUTHORITY · THE THREE NEW DIAMONDS**
> Emerald Diamond, Neo Brown Diamond, and Peacock Star Diamond emerged together on Primera. Unlike the four ruling Diamonds, they were made from the essence of all the Deity Diamonds. Emerald could sense and speak to technology. Neo Brown was a nervous, hooded historian who began the Grand Universal Timeline. Peacock Star was an artist and engineer. Emerald and Peacock Star were meant to share Court Delta, the first court with two Diamonds, and Neo Brown was given Court Neo to keep the empire's history.

**MINT INDUSTRIES · THE RESTRICTED SECTOR**
> Under Mint Industries lay the Restricted Sector, where Mint Green Diamond kept everything she did not want seen. It held a machine that forced gems to fuse whether they could or not, cells of failed and unstable results, a cold vault of stasis tubes for the experiments that went wrong, and workshops for illegal weapons. Behind a hidden wall was her decay vault, built for the four ship cores she was making for STORM, because she never fully trusted them.

**BROADCAST · THE GEMS OF THE ELDER ONES**
> A cult banned from forty-seven systems. Its members claimed the Diamonds were only fingers on the hand of a true deity called Universe Diamond, that gems were her shed skin, and that perfect gems had existed before Black Diamond. One of them, a Chrysocolla, said all of this on the Crystal Hour. Lady Crimson shattered her gem on air and called it a bit.

**PROPHECY · SAPPHIRE 8XQ'S VISION**
> The Homeworld Times weathergem had a vision on live air the night before the coronation, and it came out as verse. Three young ones made from the essence of past Eras would claim their place. Two would build new machines, one in emerald and her twin in green. The third would build a great archive. Then a servant would spill crimson wine, a keeper would feel her hours running out, and a shadow would fall across the plain. She could not say what it meant.

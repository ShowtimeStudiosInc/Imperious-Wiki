# TIMELINE III · APOCALYPTIC REVELRIES

> Records kept by the Ethereal Authority, and a few that reached it from Earth. They describe how that timeline worked rather than what happened in it. The events are on the Timeline III page.

**THE GODDESS · UNIVERSE DIAMOND**
> Universe Diamond was the Goddess of Homeworld. To anyone who met her she seemed kind, motherly, and perfect, but almost no one did. In this timeline she had withdrawn from ruling long ago and appeared only at special events.
>
> She kept Homeworld's core inside her own body, and the planet lived because she did. She could see through the eyes of any Diamond or any gem whenever she wished. Lasers from her fingertips could poof a gem without shattering it, and a second setting healed cracks and some corruption. When she let go of her aura, every gem near her felt as if they were about to poof on the spot.
>
> She called her gems her shining, twinkling little stars.

**THE GODDESS · HER SANCTUM**
> Universe lived in a Sanctum above Homeworld's palace: rooms of clouds and old stone columns, with crystals drifting through the air that may have been gems she worked on long ago. Her Zirconia, Celestia, guarded the door. Celestia was one of the four Site Directors of Universe's agency, with three pairs of seraph wings and small planets circling her neck and wings, and she called Universe her mother.

**THE AUTHORITY · THE HEAVENLY DIAMONDS**
> The Diamonds below Universe took their titles from the nine choirs of angels.
> Emerald Diamond, the Seraphim of Innovation. Technology, weapons, and expansion. Proud of her work to the point of shattering anyone who touched it without asking, and afraid above all of being forgotten. She could command machines, metals, and liquids, and run fifty holopads at once.
> Cherub Gold Diamond, the All Seeing Cherubim. Manager of the lower Diamonds and of Homeworld itself. Charming and friendly to the other Diamonds, and always watching them. His touch could turn a gem into a gold statue, his eyes could hypnotize, and wherever a gold light shone, he was looking through it.
> Celeste Diamond, the All Knowing Throne. Supreme Judge, diplomat, and keeper of Homeworld's daily problems. Loud, kind, and loyal. She knew when a gem was lying, could speak mind to mind, and sometimes saw how a situation would end.
> Bubblegum Diamond, the Dominion of Harmony. Resource gathering, builders, and terraformers. Bubbly, merciful, and led by her heart.
> Coral Reef Diamond, the Virtue of Fluidity. Exploration and colonizing. She could send her mind out of her body to find new planets, and command water.
> Nebula Diamond, the Radiant Power. Power production and the armies. She controlled plasma and valued knowledge, respect, and power above all else.
> Silver Grey Diamond, the Principality of Security. The quietest Diamond and the most violent. He placed copies of himself on every colony, hid eye sentries everywhere, and swore he would defend Homeworld until he shattered.
> Centenary Diamond, the Archangel of Ornate Dominion. Servants, Pearls, Spinels, and events. Gracious in public and selfish in private. She could restore cracked and shattered gems, and anyone who decorated badly for her lost control of their own body.
> The ninth choir, the Angels, belonged to the Cubic Zirconias.

**THE AUTHORITY · THE COURTS**
> Court Universe was an agency in which every gem was equal and free to pick a division. Court Cherub Gold watched everyone and forgave little. Court Celeste took in Off-Colors and gems who did not fit, as long as they were kind and kept the rules. Court Nebula demanded discipline and shattered in the worst cases. Court Bubblegum had loose deadlines, parties, and free speech. Court Silver Grey ran like an army, and slackers were bubbled or shattered. Court Coral Reef ran on puzzles, riddles, and a short temper. Court Centenary had room only for Pyropes and Scapolites. In Court Emerald, every gem worked in technology, weapons, or expansion, and once a century the best inventors were chosen at the Great Centennial Tech Expo on Zeta 5.

**THE AUTHORITY · THE CUBIC ZIRCONIAS**
> A Diamond was made by injecting a planet with Diamond Essence until its core became a Paragon Core, then injecting that core somewhere empty. All of it made a higher Diamond. Ninety percent made a Diamond and one Cubic Zirconia beside her, and eighty percent made a Diamond and two. A Diamond and her Zirconias were siblings, born depending on each other, and every Diamond's emergence left a crater.
>
> Madam Gold, the Angel of Radiance, was Cherub Gold's Zirconia. She could fly, and she made golden fire that healed others when she cried real tears. Her duty was to guard the loyal and judge the rule breakers. Mr. Cobalt, the Angel of Lies, could sway a gem's feelings and read the memories in any gemstone he touched.

**THE AUTHORITY · UNIVERSE'S AGENCY**
> Beneath Homeworld and its palace, Universe ran a secret agency that the rest of the Authority never knew existed. Most of its members were Era 0 gems, the first gems ever made and the ancestors of every modern Gem-Type. They shared a hivemind, could take the shape of any gem, and lived disguised in ordinary society, where they passed as rank 1.2 gems. They disliked being praised.
>
> Every agent was equal except the four Site Directors and Universe herself. The agency had five divisions: Field Agents and Hunters, Medical, Engineering, Intel Gathering, and the Librarians. A daily leaderboard ranked them, and the Hunters competed hardest. Among the Hunters were Ruby Zoisites, compound gems with two types sharing one gemstone, who fought demons with red and green flames.

**THE AUTHORITY · SMALLER RECORDS**
> Off-Color and defective were not the same thing. An Off-Color only had the wrong color for her type. A defective gem could not do the job she was made for, like a Sapphire who saw the past instead of the future.
>
> Club Ethereal was where aristocrats and authority gems went to relax, watch the entertainers, and drink the finest light-based drinks in the empire.
>
> The Diamonds kept a device called the Almighty. Sent to a sun, it aged the star until it went supernova within about an hour, destroying the whole system. It was meant only for emergencies.
>
> The ranking system the empire lived by was written by Cherub Gold Diamond, not by Universe.

**HEAVEN · THE ANGELIC HEART AND THE ANGEL CORE**
> The Angelic Heart floated high in Homeworld's Cathedral, out of reach of nearly every Diamond. It gave wings to true believers and connected every gem to Eden.
>
> Behind it was the Angel Core. Gems who bore the Mark of the Universe and followed the faith could, for a short time, connect to it. When they did, their human disguise burned away, a halo and wings of light appeared, and they could strike down evil with celestial light that hurt demons more the more they had sinned.

**EARTH · THE UNITED PROVINCES OF THE WORLD**
> Earth's history split from ours at the Library of Alexandria, which never burned. Steam power came about nine hundred years early. Steam trains crossed the old world by 400 CE, and the Vikings reached the New World by 900. In 1054 humans found the crashed Red Eye, and the Church kept it secret for nearly nine hundred years. A plague swept the world in the 1300s and left humans with modern medicine and their first vaccine. Then came the Great Relaxing, four centuries when progress slowed almost to a stop, though humans still reached the Moon in 1845.
>
> When the Church revealed the Red Eye, the world united as the United Provinces of the World. The Church governed most of the east, the UPOW proper held the central south, Oceania, and the Americas, and the Republic held the rest. When gems began falling from the sky, humans called them Skin Shifters, and the UPOW answered with the Great Bounce, a push to rebuild by blending human and gem technology.

**SCRIPTURE · THE WELL BURIED PROPHECY**
> Only gems who dug deep into Homeworld's libraries ever found this. It is told in eight Revelations.
> One: the firstborn of the Universe will enter the world and break seven seals.
> Two: at the first seal, one of the lower angels rebels. The symbol of divine majesty is cut down, Homeworld falls silent for half an hour, and the first trumpet destroys a third of the habitable worlds. War is set loose.
> Three: at the second seal, the angels fall to earth, lose their wings, and lose their way home. The second trumpet brings a blinding light and fire into the seas, turning a third of them to blood. Famine is set loose.
> Four: at the third seal, a great star falls and brings a plague that sickens mortals and drains the light from gems. Pestilence is set loose.
> Five: at the fourth seal, a third of the stars and the moon go dark, witnesses rise, and the dead return for revenge. Death is set loose.
> Six: at the fifth seal, smoke covers the sky and swarms of insects torment everyone without the Mark of the Universe for five months. The sun goes dark, the moon turns red, and mountains and islands are moved. At the sixth seal, the traitor angel gives birth to a Nephilim, who fights to stop the end and fails.
> Seven: at the sixth trumpet, the three great Seraphs hunt the child, the angels may return home, and the Mother is slain where she hides.
> Eight: at the seventh seal, the cage of the King of Demons opens, and the last battle begins.

**SCRIPTURE · THE SEVEN AND THE MOTHER**
> Lilith was the Mother of the corrupted gems, the ones called demons. She was one of the Devils, though she stood for no sin herself. As Universe's firstborn, she believed everything Universe had should be hers too, and she knew she could never measure up. She hated that Universe gave gems free will.
>
> To spread chaos she made the Seven Devils, gems with rare gifts meant to outshine the angels: Pride, Greed, Gluttony, Envy, Wrath, Sloth, and Lust.

**POEM · HOW SHE FEELS, BY EVELYN HART**
> Found between books in libraries across the empire. Whoever left it never answered a single question about it.
>
> It begins in silence. A child wakes for the first time and finds nothing on either side, only her other half behind her, and a mysterious man walking away.
>
> She is a Creator who does not know what to create. She thinks for so long she loses track of time, and when she looks up from her great creation, her other half is gone. She cries for eons and no one hears. She finds a blue planet, makes a man, and makes him a woman. The woman does not like the man and leaves, and builds an army of demons, so the Creator casts her out and makes angels to watch over what she made.
>
> She is a Gardener who grows her crops from what has died. The mysterious figure comes back, and escapes again. Storm clouds gather past her fence, so she builds one great machine to tend the garden. While her back is turned it builds seven more, and together they strip the whole garden to grow a single crop. She throws them out of the garden and makes them one with the crop they looked down on.
>
> She is a Mentor whose students mostly do not listen. One group feels the divine and works harder. Another fights her lesson. Of the last group, only one learns, and that one is willing to teach the others. For the first time in a long time, she smiles.
>
> She is a God who watches her people fall into sin. She sees her first creation crawl out of Hell and has an idea. She writes a prophecy and sends a Herald to carry it out, and watches her people suffer, sorry for it but certain it is for their own good.
>
> And the storm clouds have come closer.

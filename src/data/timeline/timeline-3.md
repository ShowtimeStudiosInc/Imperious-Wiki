# TIMELINE III


> This is the timeline that came two resets before the current one. Everything on this page happened, but only here. Universe Diamond reset the universe at the end of it, and nothing below carries into any later timeline unless a later page says so outright. Two timelines came before this one. Almost nothing from them survives.

**GENESIS**
> At the start of everything, someone the records only call [Redacted] stepped through a portal from another universe and left a single gemstone behind. Hours later it began to glow and spin, and Universe Diamond came out of it. Her birth was the Big Bang. Matter and antimatter poured out from where she stood, and she was left alone at the center of it all with no idea what she was for. Two hundred million years later, [Redacted] came back. Universe chased the only other being she had ever seen, but they got away and never returned.
>
> In time she built a people of her own. In this timeline, gems are her creation and she is their Goddess, worshipped openly across the empire she rules, the Ethereal Authority. She keeps Homeworld's core inside her own body, which is what keeps the planet alive. She can see through the eyes of any of her gems, heal cracks with a beam from her fingertips, and crush a room full of gems by letting her aura loose. Almost no one has ever seen her in person.

**THE HEAVENLY DIAMONDS**
> Universe stopped ruling day to day long ago. The Diamonds below her took titles from the choirs of angels, and every gem in the empire knew them by those titles:
> Emerald Diamond, the Seraphim of Innovation, in charge of technology, weapons, and expansion. She was the first Diamond Universe made in this timeline, and the only one who could visit her whenever she liked. After Emerald, Universe withdrew and handed every new Diamond's Paragon Core to Emerald to bring to life.
> Cherub Gold Diamond, the All Seeing Cherubim, the eyes and the fist of Homeworld. His golden lights watched every corner of the empire, and he wrote the ranking system the empire lived by.
> Celeste Diamond, the All Knowing Throne, the Supreme Judge and the empire's diplomat. She could tell when a gem was lying, and she called every gem "starlight."
> Bubblegum Diamond, the Dominion of Harmony, who ran the builders and the terraformers.
> Coral Reef Diamond, the Virtue of Fluidity, who searched the stars for new worlds.
> Nebula Diamond, the Radiant Power, who ran power production and commanded the armies.
> Silver Grey Diamond, the Principality of Security, who guarded every colony through copies of himself.
> Centenary Diamond, the Archangel of Ornate Dominion, who made the empire's servants and ran its events.
>
> Below the Diamonds were the Cubic Zirconias, who held the last choir, the Angels. A Diamond and her Zirconia came from the same Paragon Core and were born as siblings. Cherub Gold's was Madam Gold, a guardian against the empire's rule breakers.
>
> Under Homeworld itself sat something the Diamonds never knew about: Universe's own court, a secret agency made up mostly of Era 0 gems. They shared a hivemind, could take the shape of any gem, and lived disguised among ordinary society, where they were made to look like rank 1.2 gems. Within the agency, every gem was equal except the four Site Directors who led it. One of them was Celestia, Universe's own Zirconia.

**HOW THE LAST TIMELINE ENDED**
> The lore of this timeline remembers how the one before it ended. Universe had revealed herself to Homeworld at the biggest ball of that Era. Soon after, a group of Diamonds turned on her and stormed her palace. One of them, Coral, had second thoughts at the door, and the others poofed her for it. The fight lasted an hour. Then Universe let go of her aura and crushed every rebel Diamond in the room.
>
> She decided to start over, and to avoid making Diamonds like those again. With Emerald Diamond and Gold Yellow Diamond, the Head Diamond of that timeline, standing on her hands as conduits for the power, she turned time back. Almost no one kept their memories. What follows is the timeline that came after.

**THE NEW DIAMONDS AND LADY JUSTICE**
> This time, the empire started with three Diamonds: the Cherub, the Seraphim, and the Throne. They were buried in work. One day an anonymous message told them how to make more Diamonds. They found an empty planet, filled it with every Paragon Core they had, and new Diamonds rose from it. The coronation that followed was the largest event in the empire's memory.
>
> Universe was not there. She was far away on a human colony, standing in a town square disguised as a stone statue of Lady Justice, and the miracles she worked there were passed down through generations of humans.

**THE HUMANS**
> Humans in this timeline had a different history from the one we know. The Library of Alexandria never burned. Steam power came centuries early, and by 900 CE humans had reached the New World. Around 1054, they found a crashed gem ship, a Red Eye, with a mural of a radiant figure inside. The Catholic Church hid it, called it a gift from God, and its highest cardinals wore the ship's Rubies as rings.
>
> When the Church finally revealed the ship centuries later, the world united as the United Provinces of the World, and its technology leapt ahead. Humans reached the Moon in 1845. By the time the gems arrived, humanity had a colony of its own, Xenon 93B6.
>
> Universe had been walking among humans the whole time. Long ago she had posed as a scholar at the Library of Alexandria and hidden pieces of a scripture among its scrolls, written in a language no human could read. The Church matched that language to the writing inside the Red Eye, and the race to find the rest of the scripture began.

**THE PROPHECY**
> The scripture was a prophecy of the end. Gems had a copy too, buried so deep in Homeworld's libraries that almost no one had ever read it. It said that the firstborn of the Universe would enter the world and break seven seals. Each seal would bring a trumpet blast and a disaster, and the first four would set loose the Four Calamities: War, Famine, Pestilence, and Death. One of the lower angels would turn against the kingdom. The gems would lose their wings and fall to the world below. Those who bore the Mark of the Universe would be spared the worst of it. At the very end, the cage of the King of Demons would open.
>
> On Earth, a fifth piece turned up carved into the Colosseum, and a short war broke out over who controlled the scripture. Nebula Diamond's Pearl, Acurite, found the gem copy in Homeworld's Central Library. She was too late.

**MADAM GOLD**
> At Club Ethereal, Madam Gold shared a table with Centenary Diamond. Centenary laughed at low-ranked gems and said she would shatter even a defective Diamond or Zirconia. Madam Gold had spent time on Earth, had taken a human into her elite guard, and had begun to see things differently. She called Centenary a monster and decided to leave the empire.
>
> Before she could, Universe called her in. Madam Gold expected the worst and found a kind, grandmotherly figure who served her tea. Universe told her the world order was not her design, that the other Diamonds were only following the instincts they were made with, and that she should wait until after "the apocalypse" before making any choice. Then she gave her a gift: the power to take a human form. On the way out, Madam Gold met Celestia, three pairs of wings and planets circling her shoulders, guarding the door.
>
> The talk shows called Madam Gold a traitor. She went to her brother, Cherub Gold, and told him they had all been wrong, but said the rest would have to come from Universe. Cherub had never once been spoken to by Universe. The cracks on his face, already there from years of pressure, began to spread.

**THE FIRST SEAL**
> Things fell apart quickly after that. A shatterer slipped into a crew at Andromeda Station and killed most of them. A rebel community took root in the wilds of Xenon 93B6. Cherub Gold struck Emerald's gem with liquid gold as a warning to slow her weapons work, and in the end she was frozen in gold.
>
> Then, at a public event in Homeworld's town square, Cherub Gold shattered where he stood. A golden barrier rose around his remains. Homeworld went silent for half an hour. Then the first trumpet sounded across the universe. A third of the inhabited worlds were destroyed, and two beings broke into reality: War, out of Purgatory, and the Monolith, out of Hell. The first seal was broken. The lower angel who turned against the kingdom was Madam Gold.

**THE FALL**
> At Homeworld's Cathedral floated the Angelic Heart, the artifact that gave wings to the faithful. Pyrope, a gem who believed she was made to bring the end of the world, walked in disguised as a White Sapphire beside Celeste Diamond, who had come to pray. While Celeste prayed, a mark of two doves appeared on her forehead. In the next room, Pyrope's brother Lucius was pouring darkness into the Angelic Heart.
>
> A hologram of Universe appeared over every part of Homeworld. Someone had defiled the Heart, she said, and so every gem had lost its connection to Eden. They would fall to the world of those they had looked down on. Homeworld's surface folded into a ship, threw every gem out into space, and vanished. Portals swallowed them all and dropped them on Xenon 93B6 and on Earth, powerless, unable to shapeshift more than once, and barely able to speak to the humans around them. The angels had lost their wings.

**THE SECOND AND THIRD SEALS**
> Lucius looked like a silent child, but he was the Trumpet Bearer. He blew the second trumpet into his fist. The sky went white for half an hour, then fire fell into the seas of every living world, and a third of the oceans turned red.
>
> He traveled with Jack McGuffin, a human detective who called him son and believed in Universe's plan. They had already killed War themselves. They took Celeste and Celestial White Sapphire along with them, disguised as humans. Celeste went by Estelle Itri. In a town on the edge of a plain, Lucius burned the mayor's office down with the mayor inside, then blew the third trumpet. A star fell and brought a plague with it. Humans fell sick, and gems turned green and cold as their light drained away. Lucius told Celeste the Diamonds were false angels, and that the true ones were still out there, watching over anyone who sought redemption.
>
> On Earth, gems fell from the sky in fire. Humans called them Skin Shifters, and paranoia spread. Eight revelations appeared carved into the eight tallest mountains on Earth, with the last on Everest. Humanity began rebuilding, mixing gem technology with its own, and made ready to stand against whoever was writing their fate across their land: Universe.

**EDEN**
> Universe returned to the empty site where Homeworld had stood and rebuilt it. Around Cherub Gold's remains she raised Yggdrasil, a tree so large its leaves covered much of the planet, and bound a gem named Eden to it as the planet's avatar. The new Homeworld was called Eden.
>
> Gems who had lived in Eden began turning up among the rebels on Xenon 93B6. The rebels took them in despite everything, and some of them received the Mark of the Universe for it. They shared a vision: five leaders would die, Death would walk the land, a child called the Nephilim would be born to the traitor angel and try to save everyone and fail, and the Mother of all demons would be slain.

**REDEMPTION**
> Some of the fallen started to change. Silver Grey Diamond crashed into a kindergarten on Xenon 93B6, his wings gone, and took a human form called Seth. A human named Dean Winchester and his wife Bernadette sheltered him. With Sunni Mayflower, the first Sunstone in human form, they rebuilt a ruined church on Earth as the Church of Universe, a refuge for gems and humans. Seth came to see that ruling his court by fear had been his sin.
>
> The plague, it turned out, could be cured by redemption. Not everyone made it. Centenary Diamond was close to starting down that road when one of her Pearls screamed at her, and she shattered herself. Demons came for the church. Sunni, carrying the Mark of the Universe, linked to the Angel Core for the first time, grew wings of light, and drove them off. One of them was still trying to break the next seal.
>
> Universe, meanwhile, walked Earth as a woman named Vivienne Hale. She healed Dean and gave him the Ring of War, which held the dead Calamity's power.

**HOW SHE FEELS**
> Late in this timeline, a poem began turning up between books in libraries, signed by someone named Evelyn Hart. It described a creator who woke alone beside her other half, lost them, and cried for eons. She made a man and a woman, and the woman rebelled and raised an army of demons. She planted a garden and built a machine to tend it, and the machine built seven more and drained the garden to grow a single crop, so she threw them down among the crop they had looked down on. In the end, she watched her people fall into sin, wrote a prophecy, and sent a herald to carry it out. She knew they would suffer and believed it was for their own good. And beyond her fence, storm clouds were gathering.

**THE END OF TIMELINE III**
> The storm cloud approached.....
>
> And the whole garden had disappeared.

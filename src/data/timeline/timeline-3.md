# TIMELINE III

> This is the timeline that came two resets before the current one. Everything on this page happened, but only here. Universe Diamond reset the universe at the end of it, and nothing below carries into any later timeline unless a later page says so outright. Two timelines came before this one. Almost nothing from them survives.
>
> This page follows events in the order they happened. The Ethereal Authority's records, its Diamonds, its courts, the prophecy, and the poem, are kept in the Imperial Library.

**THE BEGINNING · GENESIS**
> At the start of everything, someone the records only call [Redacted] stepped through a portal from another universe and left a single gemstone behind. Hours later it began to glow and spin, and Universe Diamond came out of it. Her birth was the Big Bang. Two hundred million years later, [Redacted] came back. Universe chased the only other being she had ever seen, but they got away and never returned.
>
> In time she made a people of her own. Gems were her creation and she was their Goddess, and her empire was the Ethereal Authority.

**THE BEGINNING · ACROSS HUMAN HISTORY**
> Long before the gems found them, Universe walked among humans. Around 245 BC she posed as a scholar at the Library of Alexandria, under one name after another, and hid three pieces of a scripture among its scrolls in a language no human could read. More pieces she carved into stone around the world.
>
> Around 1054, humans found a crashed gem ship, a Red Eye. The Catholic Church hid it and matched the writing inside to the fragments from Alexandria, and the Pope sent his followers to find the rest. Centuries later the Church revealed the ship, and the world united as the United Provinces of the World. Humanity settled its first colony, Xenon 93B6.

**THE BEGINNING · HOW THE LAST TIMELINE ENDED**
> The lore of this timeline remembers how the one before it ended. Universe had revealed herself to Homeworld at the biggest ball of that Era. Soon after, a group of Diamonds turned on her and stormed her palace. One of them, Coral, had second thoughts at the door, and the others poofed her for it. The fight lasted an hour. Then Universe let go of her aura and crushed every rebel Diamond in the room.
>
> She decided to start over, and to avoid making Diamonds like those again. With Emerald Diamond and Gold Yellow Diamond, the Head Diamond of that timeline, standing on her hands as conduits for the power, she turned time back. Almost no one kept their memories.

**BEFORE THE FALL · THE NEW DIAMONDS**
> This time, the empire started with three Diamonds: the Cherub, the Seraphim, and the Throne. Emerald was the first, and the only Diamond who could visit Universe whenever she liked. They were buried in work. One day an anonymous message told them how to make more Diamonds. They found an empty planet, filled it with every Paragon Core they had, and new Diamonds rose from it. The coronation that followed was the largest event in the empire's memory.
>
> Universe was not there. She was far away on a human colony, standing in a town square disguised as a stone statue of Lady Justice. The miracles she worked there were passed down through generations of humans, until the story reached the Vatican and a human group called MYSTIC.

**BEFORE THE FALL · SIGNS**
> Not long after the coronation, the sun of Colony 0K4e-11O went supernova and took the colony with it. On Earth, a group of evangelicals found another piece of the scripture carved into the rock near Stonehenge. Then a fifth turned up on the Colosseum, right under the Vatican's nose, and when people read it they understood their fate had been written for them. Protests followed, and a short war. The evangelicals lost within a month and handed over everything they had found.

**BEFORE THE FALL · MADAM GOLD**
> At Club Ethereal, Madam Gold shared a table with Centenary Diamond. Centenary laughed at low-ranked gems and said she would shatter even a defective Diamond or Zirconia. Madam Gold had spent time on Earth, had taken a human into her elite guard, and had begun to see things differently. She called Centenary a monster and decided to leave the empire.
>
> Before she could, Universe called her in. Madam Gold expected the worst and found a kind, grandmotherly figure who served her tea. Universe told her the world order was not her design, that the other Diamonds were only following the instincts they were made with, and that she should wait until after "the apocalypse" before making any choice. Then she gave her a gift: the power to take a human form. On the way out, Madam Gold met Celestia, Universe's Zirconia, guarding the door.
>
> A talk show aired footage of the conversation at Club Ethereal and called Madam Gold a traitor. She went to her brother, Cherub Gold, and told him they had all been wrong, but said the rest would have to come from Universe. Cherub had never once been spoken to by Universe. The cracks on his face, already there from years of pressure, began to spread. That same night, Nebula Diamond's Pearl, Acurite, found an old scripture in Homeworld's Central Library and went to warn her Diamond.

**BEFORE THE FALL · TROUBLE SPREADS**
> A crew arrived at Andromeda Station for a routine job. The power died, the crew woke up to broken systems, and one of them was a shatterer. Only one gem survived. Days later, a large rebel community appeared in the wilds of Xenon 93B6.
>
> Emerald came to visit Universe for the first time in Cycles, full of news about her colonies and her plan to grow giant trees in space and drop them on planets. Universe cut her short: gems were breaking into her laboratory. Cherub Gold had his own answer to how fast Emerald was building weapons. He struck her gem with liquid gold as a warning.

**BEFORE THE FALL · HOW SHE FEELS**
> Around this time, a poem began turning up in the empire's libraries, tucked between the books on the shelves. It was signed by someone named Evelyn Hart and titled "How SHE Feels." It told the story of a creator who lost her other half, made a garden, watched it go wrong, and finally wrote a prophecy and sent a herald to carry it out. Nobody knew who had written it, and nobody ever answered questions about it. Its full text is kept in the Imperial Library.

**THE FIRST SEAL · CHERUB GOLD SHATTERS**
> At a public event in Homeworld's town square, Cherub Gold shattered where he stood, from the strain alone. A golden barrier rose around his remains and threw his own Pearl back when she ran to him. Homeworld went silent for half an hour.
>
> Then the first trumpet sounded across the universe. The next day, a third of the inhabited worlds and part of Homeworld's colonies were simply gone. The first seal was broken. One of the lower angels had turned against the kingdom, just as the prophecy said, and that angel was Madam Gold.
>
> On Xenon 93B6, in an underground base she had built long ago, Pyrope heard the news and was delighted.

**THE FIRST SEAL · WAR AND THE MONOLITH**
> Emerald was found frozen in gold, and soon after, her Pearl's word reached Celeste: Emerald was dead. A barrier rose over a sector of Homeworld, and two beings broke into reality. War came out of Purgatory, and the Monolith came out of Hell. Silver Diamond's forces were stretched so thin that he told Celeste the Cathedral was the only place he could still keep safe.
>
> War found Pyrope's base, and Pyrope sent her after Madam Gold. For the first time in Cycles, Universe left her Sanctum. She took a human form, Vivienne Hale, and went to the human worlds with her Sapphire, Andromeda, who now called herself Annabelle Butler.

**THE FALL · THE ANGELIC HEART**
> Pyrope's true name was Lilith. She was Universe's firstborn, who had rebelled against her and raised an army of demons, and she believed she was made to bring the end of the world. The corrupted gems were her children.
>
> While War kept Madam Gold busy, Pyrope walked into Homeworld's Cathedral disguised as a White Sapphire, right beside Celeste Diamond, who had come to pray. While Celeste prayed, a mark of two doves appeared on her forehead. In the next room, Pyrope's brother Lucius was pouring darkness into the Angelic Heart, the artifact that gave wings to the faithful.
>
> A hologram of Universe appeared over every part of Homeworld. Someone had defiled the Heart, she said, and so every gem had lost its connection to Eden. They would fall to the world of those they had looked down on. Homeworld's surface folded into a ship, threw every gem out into space, and vanished. Portals swallowed them all and dropped them on Xenon 93B6 and on Earth, powerless, unable to shapeshift more than once, and barely able to speak to the humans around them.

**AFTER THE FALL · THE SECOND SEAL**
> Celeste woke up in the Malice Kindergarten with no powers, and beside her was the gem who had caused all of it. Lucius looked like a silent child and spoke through words on a holoscreen. He told her he was a guardian, that he had never needed the Angelic Heart for his powers, and that his big sister was named Lilith. Celeste had never heard the name, and had no idea she had already met her. She chose to trust him anyway. They took human forms, and she went by Estelle Itri. On the way they found Celestial White Sapphire hiding in a hole, as powerless and as frightened as they were, and blind.
>
> The next day, Lucius blew the second trumpet into his fist. The sky went white for half an hour, then fire fell into the seas of every living world. A third of the oceans turned red, and a third of the ships in the sea and in space were lost.

**AFTER THE FALL · THE STRANDED**
> Silver Grey Diamond came down on Xenon 93B6 as a falling angel. His wings withered, he crashed into a kindergarten, and he could no longer speak any language but Angelic. Sunni Mayflower, the first Sunstone, found him there with a handful of lost gems. They took human forms, and Silver became a tall, limping man named Seth. A human scientist named Lucy took the stranded gems into her laboratory, along with Coral Reef Diamond, Centenary Diamond, and their servants.
>
> Lucius, meanwhile, set a wrecked car alight with hellfire and turned it into a limousine with a shade at the wheel. In the back of it, he leaned over and whispered something to Celeste that no gem had ever been told: Heaven was the core of Homeworld. It was at Her core.

**AFTER THE FALL · PYROPE ATTACKS**
> Silver went out with Sunni to look for Celeste and ran straight into Pyrope's magenta warship and her swarm of corrupted gems. A human named Dean Davidson was driving home from the city of New Hurricane when he saw the fight, and he pulled them all into his car. His mansion was already covered in crosses and notes on the revelations, and he had stopped sleeping trying to understand them. Dean and his wife Bernadette, a military general, took the gems in. Sunni told him the truth: they were the angels, and the pink gem was letting loose the Four Horsemen.
>
> On Xenon 93B6, every piece of the scripture finally came together at a Vatican outpost. The governments of Earth and the colony decided the apocalypse had already reached its third revelation, and the press began broadcasting the scriptures to everyone.

**THE THIRD SEAL · PESTILENCE**
> In New Hurricane, a woman named Eleanor gave Celestial White Sapphire back her sight. Then the limo drove to the edge of a small town on a plain. A woman stepped out of the woods to meet it. It was Vivienne Hale, and she petted Lucius like a child and told him the mayor was at Town Hall. Lucius walked in, burned every holy symbol in the building, pinned the mayor to the wall, and left the office in hellfire.
>
> Back in the limo, he blew the third trumpet. A star fell and brought a plague with it. Humans fell sick, and gems turned green and cold as their light drained away. Lucius wrote out the words "I am the Trumpet Bearer," and told Celeste the Diamonds were false angels. The true ones were still out there, he said, watching over anyone who sought redemption.
>
> That same day, Universe returned to the empty site where Homeworld had stood and rebuilt it. Around Cherub Gold's remains she raised Yggdrasil, a tree so large its leaves covered much of the planet, and bound a gem named Eden to it as the planet's avatar. The new Homeworld was called Eden.

**THE THIRD SEAL · THE DEATH OF WAR**
> Lucius did not travel alone. Jack McGuffin, a human detective, drove with him and called him son. Pyrope and two more Calamities, Famine and a masked plague doctor named Pestilence, watched what the two of them did to War: they pinned her to a wall, carved a crown into her chest, and ripped out her gem and shattered it.
>
> Famine and Pestilence came for Dean's house next. They blamed the gems for War's death and dragged Dean away to lure Silver out. As Sunni wept, she saw a figure with golden eyes, great wings, and a spear watching her through the window.

**THE THIRD SEAL · SIGNS ON EARTH**
> The news from Xenon 93B6 got worse. Demons spread in numbers nobody had seen before, and the Monolith was behind it. An alien ship dropped demons on New Hurricane. Someone sent the press the location of a mural in an abandoned building, showing five figures under five stars. A new star appeared in the night sky, and people called it the Star of Hope.
>
> On Earth, a massive earthquake leveled the Bengal basin, and then the eight revelations appeared carved into the eight tallest mountains, the last on Everest. Humanity began rebuilding, mixing gem technology with its own, and made ready to stand against whoever was writing their fate across their land: Universe.

**THE THIRD SEAL · EDEN'S FALLEN**
> In Pyrope's base, a holy strike gathered overhead and was called off. Pyrope let Dean go. He was found by Vivienne Hale, who took him to Eden, healed his wounds with a snap, and gave him the Ring of War, which held the dead Calamity's power. Then she walked him home.
>
> Gems who had lived in Eden began turning up among the rebels on Xenon 93B6. The rebels took them in despite everything, and some of them received the Mark of the Universe for it. They shared a vision: five leaders would die, Death would walk the land, a child called the Nephilim would be born to the traitor angel and try to save everyone and fail, and the Mother of all demons would be slain.

**THE THIRD SEAL · WINTER HILL**
> On a mountain road, Lucius's limo was run off the edge. An archangel named Gabriel pulled everyone out of the wreck. He told Jack that he did not agree with what Universe had the boy doing, and that one of the Calamities would come to help them the next day.
>
> They went on to Winter Hill, Lucius's home town on Earth. There he was known as the son of Senator Wagner and the only survivor of the fire at Dante Manor, and the townspeople whispered that he had killed his parents. Jack pinned a picture of the five stars mural to his case board. He believed the next seals depended on finding five leaders who fit it. Lucius photographed and took notes on every person in town, then walked into Town Hall and took hold of the judge's mind.

**THE THIRD SEAL · THE CHURCH OF UNIVERSE**
> The plague, it turned out, could be cured by redemption. Dean, Seth, and Sunni rebuilt a ruined church on Earth as the Church of Universe, a refuge for gems and humans alike. Seth came to see that ruling his court by fear had been his sin. He had never been loved by anyone before Dean.
>
> Not everyone made it. Centenary Diamond was close to starting down that road when one of her Pearls screamed at her, and she shattered herself. The Pearl did the same. Demons came for the church. Sunni, carrying the Mark of the Universe, linked to the Angel Core for the first time, grew wings of light, and drove them off. One of them was still trying to break the next seal.

**THE END OF TIMELINE III**
> The storm cloud approached.....
>
> And the whole garden had disappeared.

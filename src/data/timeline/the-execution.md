# THE EXECUTION

**RECAP**
> That evening, the whole Agency gathered at the Training Grounds inside the Bio-Dome. A full training field had been filled with seats, and a raised platform was set aside for officials. Alpha Black Diamond, known to most of the Agency as Director General Vivienne, arrived early, as she always does. With her was a nervous Kyanite named Kassidy, there on Vivienne's invitation.
>
> While the seats filled, the two of them talked. Vivienne said she was worried about Emerald Diamond. In her view, Emerald cared far too much about what others thought of her, had grown distrustful of the other Diamonds, and kept getting baited by one of Nightshade's Cubic Zirconia, Grand Regent Reina. Kassidy agreed, but hoped Emerald would find her footing. Vivienne also mentioned she would soon visit Nightshade and Maroon, since she had a gift for Maroon. Kassidy assumed she was here for a trial. Vivienne corrected her. The trial had happened long ago, and the prisoner had been on death row ever since. This was a public execution, and the crimes would be read out first: mass murder, treason against both the Gempire and the United Frontier, torture, betrayal of asylum, impersonating an officer, espionage, poisoning a kindergarten's essence supply, theft of classified information, unauthorized fusion, and shattering several Agency agents, among many more. Then, on a whim, Vivienne changed into a brand new Diamond form, still shrunk down, with a striking dress and free-flowing hair like a cloud of cosmic gas.
>
> Her daughters arrived next. Ophan's Ink came first and could barely hide how excited they were for the execution. Cherubs' Raven followed. The third daughter, Seraph's Obsidian, never showed up, and Vivienne grew more worried as the hours passed. Madame Seraphine, the Agency's librarian, slipped in beside the table. Her book club had been cancelled for the event.
>
> Down in the general seating, Cyrus arrived with Ash, who was in a new body and stopping to touch the grass and feel everything. Wasp showed up too, having run over right after checking on Cream. Cyrus told them who the prisoner was: a high-ranking member of STORM. Ash spotted Vivienne's new look and asked if pearls could hold power in Alpha Black's court. Cyrus told her that the "pearls" up there were Alpha Black's daughters. Hathor, a blind Black Star Corundum, walked in under a ring of circling crows. Kassidy's Lemon Quartz guard, Leo, came running in late and upset, because an Emerald had just cussed him out.
>
> Then a squad of gems dragged a massive chained prisoner down the center aisle to the stage. Vivienne told the table that the executioner would be her brother. A dark portal tore open in the air, and through it came the sounds of gems screaming and groaning. Grand Regent Dante stepped out, ruler of the Underworld and head of HUNTER, as large as Alpha Black herself. The whole arena went silent. Ash had worked up the courage to walk over to the officials' table and froze halfway there. Dante shrank down, helped her to her feet, and walked her the rest of the way. That is how she learned that "Vivienne" was Alpha Black Diamond.
>
> Dante greeted his nieces and his sister, then sat down for a cup of tea first. He had never heard of the new Diamonds. Ash asked why the Gempire had become so harsh, and Vivienne explained that it happened slowly. Theta Gold made serial numbers because he didn't have time to name every gem. Epsilon Blue added fusion licenses, which were sensible enough, to keep anything from growing into a Leviathan Class fusion. Then the current ruling Diamonds collapsed the economy that had stood for two Eras and built the caste system. Now they write the laws as a council, Nightshade enforces them, and Epsilon is too buried in paperwork to repeal them. Vivienne herself was busy guarding both the Gempire and the United Frontier from hostile groups, and STORM was one of them. She also told Ash that her real name is Vivienne, and that every gem in the Agency and the United Frontier has a real name, not a serial number.
>
> Dante finished his tea and vanished into a shroud of darkness, reappearing on the stage at his full height. The guards released the prisoner, who immediately tried to run. With a tap of his foot, ghostly hands tore up out of the ground and pinned them in place for everyone to see. Dante splashed them with a gray sludge that stripped away every one of their abilities. A young WARD recruit tried to hide behind a taller operative, and a veteran steered her right back into view. The old HUNTER veterans watched with crossed arms, like they had waited years for this.
>
> When parts of the crowd started losing interest, Dante opened his coat. Inside it was a void so dark it swallowed nearly all light. Hundreds of small grasping limbs pulled the prisoner inside, where they were crushed and tormented in the dark for fifteen minutes. When the coat opened again, the STORM official came out shaking, frozen in fear, and drained of most of their color for good. After that, nobody in the crowd looked away.
>
> For the final stage, Dante pushed a ball of black, deathly energy into the prisoner's gem. They screamed for nearly forty seconds as black cracks spread through their body. Then their body came apart in three loud cracks: first a leg, then the middle, then an arm. With each one, a black substance filled in where the joint should be, like a doll being taken apart. They poofed, and a gust of wind blasted out across the crowd. Their bare gem floated over the stage. For a moment, nothing happened. Then, just as Kassidy said she was alright, it creaked and shattered into countless pieces.
>
> Alpha Black stood and walked to the stage, and the whole crowd shrank back as she passed. She thanked everyone for coming, and darkness poured out of the stage to build a temporary banquet hall, with carts of food rolling in. An INTEL handler reported that the compromised cells had been quarantined, and that with the official gone, his team could start patching the security leaks in Sector Four. Alpha had the files sent through ARCHIVE.
>
> At the table, Vivienne finally sensed where Seraph's Obsidian was. She had been at Club Imperious the whole time. Vivienne facepalmed, then decided that at least her daughter was having fun. She announced it was time for her yearly vacation, and that Dante and the Council would be in charge while she was away. Elsewhere, Ash tried food for the first time, having only ever had coolant, and Wasp kept stealing food off Cyrus's plate, promising to buy him dinner after patrol one night if he let her keep doing it. Kassidy kept telling Leo not to overeat, since the other Kyanites would ask about him.

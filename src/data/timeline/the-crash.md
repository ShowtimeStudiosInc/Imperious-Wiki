# THE CRASH

**RECAP: SO FAR**
> Night had fallen on Homeworld when a streak of cyan light crossed the sky, curved out over the Wilds, and came down a few miles from the Rogue Settlement, 230 miles south-east of the city. Idris, an old Metal walking back from the nearest kindergarten, watched it scream past him and started toward the impact. Behind him, the settlement emptied out. Guy, a Uranium, was sure it was a Diamond conspiracy. Cove, a Malachite, flew off hoping for comet shards to keep. Bellatrix packed up her medical supplies, Velveteen came out to see what the commotion was, and Odelia, an assassin who keeps to herself, stepped out of her house for once. An outsider named Loki, an Emerald, had just arrived at the settlement and drifted along with them.
>
> At the bottom of the crater sat something that looked like a space rock, far too intact for the crash it had survived. Its cracks were pitch black inside. Guy poked it. Bellatrix tapped it with her fan. Stephany, a gunslinging Steel, showed up on the crater's rim with twin revolvers and a lasso, and Idris told her to stop showing off. Then a rumbling started that didn't shake the ground. A seam opened on the back of the rock like a hatch, and light flooded out. Guy screamed that it was a parasite from the movies. Stephany lassoed him and kicked him toward the light anyway.
>
> An eight-foot figure stepped out of the smoke: Cole von Falken. Stephany kept a revolver on him and told him he was in rogue territory. Cove talked everyone down. Cole explained that he was from the United Frontier, the original Rogues, traveling the universe alone, until a Leviathan rammed his ship and sent him crashing here. He had assumed he'd landed on a Gempire colony. His ship, which he calls Princess, wasn't flying again until he could reach an old acquaintance, and for that he needed parts. Stephany told him the Bi-Colored Diamond's name was Kandy. Cole decided that was a stripper's name, and Stephany nearly choked trying not to laugh.
>
> Idris offered to take Cole to the settlement. Before leaving, Cole had the ship cloak itself as an ordinary rock. Back at the settlement, Guy was banging on doors warning that the Authority was invading, and getting doors slammed in his face. Idris calmed the crowd and sent the Bronzes to work out what Princess would need. On the walk in, they passed Neoni, a shy Uranium living in the community dumpster, who screamed at the sight of Cole and dove back into the trash. Idris apologized and offered to show Cole the fusion quarter of the settlement while the supplies were gathered.

**HIGHLIGHTS SO FAR**
> ⟡ A ship disguised as a space rock crashed a few miles from the Rogue Settlement, out in the Wilds.
> ⟡ Its pilot, Cole von Falken, is from the United Frontier. He says a Leviathan rammed his ship and sent him down here.
> ⟡ His ship, Princess, is cloaked at the crash site and can't fly until it's repaired and he reaches an old acquaintance.

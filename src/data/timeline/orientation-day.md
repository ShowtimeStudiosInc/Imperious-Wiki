# IN THE BEGINNING…

**RECAP**
> The event opens with gems from all across the courts pouring into the courtyard, so many that the streets and alleys got packed and roads had to close entirely. New arrivals like Graphene, E-Ash, Stellar Gold, Spinel, a group of Morganites (including one named 2I2E), Ruby "Ribbons," and August all showed up, most of them confused about what was going on.
>
> A holographic figure named Ava, "the Homeworld Gem User Assistance Nexus", then appeared at the podium and launched into a welcome speech. She told the new gems they were all born with purpose, and that they'd already proven themselves by helping colonize their home planet. A few gems, Graphene and E-Ash especially, got confused thinking they were being congratulated for "conquering" something, leading to some funny back and forth about what Ava meant by "conquering". Ava went on explaining the Gempire's mission is simple: grow, expand, and bring order, with every gem playing some role, whether militia, labs, aristocracy, or background work.
>
> Ava then let everyone know they'd each meet an assigned administrator for their first task before going on a full tour of Homeworld's districts. Graphene tried to make a break for it and leave but got stopped by Axinite, who told her attendance wasn't optional. After the admin meetings wrapped, everyone got funneled onto warp pads to board flying tour vehicles, with the whole cast conveniently ending up on the same one. E-Ash sat alone in the back since her leaky-oil metal smell put people off, until Graphene chose to sit with her. Morganite cheerfully introduced herself to Graphene, and Spinel found a seat nearby too, with small talk carrying them until the tour got moving.
>
> Ava guided the group through the districts one by one. District 0 was the oldest, most sacred ground, tied to Alpha Black, Theta Gold, and Epsilon Blue, where E-Ash wondered aloud who "birthed" the Deity Diamonds, and Ava explained most believe the Deity Diamonds were created alongside the universe itself. District 1 belonged to Nightshade Violet, known for constant surveillance from his all-seeing eyes, which made E-Ash uneasy, like she was back in Kandy Diamond's lab, though Morganite thought spotting the eyes was like a fun game. District 2 was Maroon Red's domain, all about militia training and discipline, where the group spotted a Hessonite, sparring in a colosseum against a hologram of herself with an unmatched win streak. District 3 was Bi-Colored Kandy Diamond's tech hub, always midnight and running on coffee, where E-Ash quietly called Kandy "a monster" while everyone else marveled at the lights, and Morganite had never even heard of coffee before.
>
> While looking down at the streets during all this, Spinel, E-Ash, and Axinite spotted a cloaked, very tall figure opening a hatch in the ground and leading strange creatures into it, clearly not part of the tour. When they looked back, there was nothing there, no figure, no creatures, not even a visible hatch. Morganite was bummed she didn't get to see where they went. A new voice then spoke up, revealed to be another passenger named Cyrus, who'd apparently been on the vehicle the whole time without anyone noticing. Cyrus suggested the group regroup later and check the alley out for themselves. Theories flew, magic, old tech, something else, but nobody landed on an answer. Wasp, a Bumblebee Jasper also riding along, agreed something odd was going on.
>
> The tour continued into District 4, Saturn Light's territory, home to restoration, coordination, and the Open Stage where performers practice. August and E-Ash both admitted they'd never have the guts to perform in front of anyone, and Morganite excitedly asked if anyone could perform there, only to be told it was for Entertainment Gems specifically, which bummed her out since her job is bubble keeping. E-Ash also asked where Ava's memory core was, but Ava said she couldn't share that. Finally, District 6, the Docking Bays and shipment hub, was the last stop, where Morganite spotted the very ship that had originally brought her to Homeworld. With that, Ava wrapped up the tour, telling everyone it was time to begin the work they were made for.
>
> Once the hovercraft landed, everyone started peeling off toward their own paths. E-Ash asked Ava to help her find her Diamond and was directed to the nearest warp pad to District 0, the Throne of Homeworld. Cyrus got off first, with Morganite trailing after him since she thought he was cool, and Wasp followed too, just glad to be back on solid ground. Cyrus quietly told the group that if they wanted to investigate the alley activity, they should meet him back at that same spot in a few days. Axinite headed off to meet the higher up he'd be serving under. Spinel slipped off to her own warp pad without much fuss. Ribbons and August also got off, Ribbons excited to investigate the alley and August walking toward the least obvious warp pad. Graphene straight up bolted before anyone could hand her any responsibilities. Cyrus and Wasp warped off together, and finally Morganite happily hopped onto her own pad to go start her first assignment, bringing the event to a close.

**HIGHLIGHTS**
> ⟡ New gems from every court gathered on Homeworld, where Ava, Homeworld's AI assistant, welcomed them and laid out the Gempire's mission: grow, expand, and bring order.
> ⟡ The group toured Homeworld's districts, from the sacred District 0 to Kandy Diamond's tech hub in District 3.
> ⟡ During the tour, Spinel, E-Ash, and Axinite saw a tall, cloaked figure lead strange creatures into a hidden hatch in District 3. A moment later, the figure, the creatures, and the hatch were all gone.
> ⟡ Cyrus, who'd been on the ride the whole time without anyone noticing, asked the group to meet him at that spot in a few days to investigate.
> ⟡ Ava wouldn't say where her memory core is.

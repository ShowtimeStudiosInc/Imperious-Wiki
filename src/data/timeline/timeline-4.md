# TIMELINE IV


> This is the timeline that came before the current one. Everything on this page happened, but only here. Universe Diamond reset the universe at the end of it, and nothing below carries into Timeline V unless a later page says so outright.

**THE GRAND BALLROOM · THE FESTIVAL OF THE FIRST LIGHT**
> Every year, Homeworld gathered in the Grand Ballroom in District 0 for the Festival of the First Light, the celebration of Alpha Black Diamond's emergence. Court Saturn organized it that year. Once seated, every gem was made the same height. Four thrones for the ruling Diamonds stood on one side of the balcony and three for the Deity Diamonds on the other. Alpha, still veiled under her hat, invited gems to come up and introduce themselves. Crimson Red Diamond arrived in a column of fire. Theta Gold and Epsilon Blue never came. When Stellar Gold used her moment to complain about how she was being worked to the bone, Alpha told her to stay close to the Sardonyx at her table and say one word: Shevvoth. That Sardonyx was Alina in disguise, and before the night was over, Alpha had decided the whole table would be moved into her own court.

**DISTRICTS 0, 1, AND 6 · THE WHITE KNIGHT**
> Halfway through the dancing, the floor shook three times. At least sixty-four corrupted gems burst up through it, with a giant worm tearing through several levels of the building. Alpha had the guests evacuated and left the problem to her court. Alina's table chased the cause to the room holding the Memory Core, where an armored gem in white, the White Knight, had already taken it. The Knight rolled itself into a ball, smashed out through a window, and fled across the Twist-About Plaza in District 1, firing on everyone who followed. At Landing Platform A in District 6, a ship was waiting. The Knight drew a sword that matched its armor and, with a single slash, poofed every gem chasing it. Then it boarded the ship with the Memory Core and left. Alina gathered up their gemstones and took them to the Agency.
>
> The next morning, the Homeworld Times reported that the Grand Ballroom was destroyed and that gems had been shattered. Court Epsilon opened an inquiry into how corrupted gems got past security. Nobody mentioned the Memory Core.

**THE FALL OF MINT GREEN DIAMOND**
> Six nights after the ball, an explosion went off in the underground labs beneath Mint Green Diamond's palace, the seat of her court and the headquarters of Mint Industries. The palace collapsed into its own foundations and burned. In the morning, Court Saturn's recovery teams found Mint Green Diamond in the wreckage, shattered. It was no accident. Someone had shattered her and let the blast hide it, and no one on Homeworld ever learned who. Court Alpha announced that plans had already been made for this, and that a new Diamond would emerge to take her place. On the Crystal Hour, Lady Crimson asked the obvious question on air: how does the most careful Diamond in the empire have an accident in the one building where she made every rule?

**THE EBONY TOWER · COURT ALPHA**
> The cast reformed in the Agency's Medical Bay, where Angelites told them they had been cut in half by a weapon made to slice through light forms. Director Alina gave them a choice: have their memories of the night wiped and go home, or join Court Alpha, also called the Agency, as Operatives. All of them joined. Rank meant nothing there; standing came from earning the respect of their peers. Alina told them plainly that the newer Diamonds had turned what the Origin Diamond built into a dictatorship, and that the result was a Rebel force too large and organized for the ruling Diamonds to stop. Its leadership included a gem from Omega White Diamond's old court. They also met Agent Grey, a white Hessonite from that same court who now led one of the Agency's other teams.

**ALPHA REEF · THE PEARL AUCTION**
> For weeks, the gossip shows had talked about the Blue Queen, a Perfect Cut Pearl whose Nacre had turned away every gem who came for her. She was moved from Apociestis Reef to Alpha Reef, the Reef closest to Homeworld, for a specialty auction run by Pricilla, an enormous Era 0 Nacre who had kept the Reef for over two billion years. Bidders didn't pay. They fought for each Pearl in a battle dimension. Plum went to the Sapphire Tulip, who fused with her Mega Ruby into a Garnet to win her, and Stellar Gold won the Imperial Topaz Pearl. The Perfect Cut Pearl was fought over in a forest of giant trees. The Agency fielded two fusions, Electrum (Cyrus and Stellar Gold) and Obsidian, a command-center fusion of a Sapphire, the Black Hessonite, two Pearls, and three Rubies. They beat Rhodolite, a Sugilite, a Spessartine, and a Bell Metal on the way to the center, where a desperate fusion absorbed its partners and became the Gaia Stone, Mother of Planets. It took everyone to bring it down. A final dice roll between Cyrus and the Agency's Head Director settled it, and the Perfect Cut Pearl joined the Agency as the Black Hessonite's aide.

**PRIMERA · THREE NEW DIAMONDS**
> On a barren field on Primera, Alpha Black watched three new Diamonds emerge: Emerald Diamond, who could sense and speak to technology; Neo Brown Diamond, a nervous, hooded historian; and Peacock Star Diamond, an artist and engineer. Unlike the four ruling Diamonds, these three were made from the essence of all the Deity Diamonds. Emerald and Peacock Star would share one court, Court Delta, the first to have two Diamonds, and inherit everything Mint Green Diamond had left behind, except her ruined palace, which the Agency was still reviewing. Neo Brown would get Court Neo and keep the empire's history. Alpha meant it as a friendly race. She carried them off-world in her Diamond Ship, a three-headed hydra built from shadow that needs no fuel and travels through portals of darkness. They waited for their coronation at Titanus Station, a station of shadow orbiting Sarnus, where Neo Brown began the Grand Universal Timeline and found that gem technology had barely moved in billions of years.
>
> On the eve of the coronation, the Homeworld Times weathergem, Sapphire 8XQ, had a vision on live air. It came out as verse: three fledglings made from the essence of past Eras, two building machines and one building an archive, and at the end, a servant spilling crimson wine, a keeper feeling her hours run out, and a shadow cast across the plain. She couldn't say what it meant.

**THE CENTRAL SPIRE, HELIODOR · THE CORONATION**
> The coronation was held in the ballroom at the top of Heliodor's Central Spire. Crowns formed over the three new Diamonds, and Alpha named them her three new hopes, meant to win back the freedoms the others had taken. Emerald, overshadowed by Peacock Star from the start, left the Spire before the night was over. In a private room, Alpha told Claret Violet Diamond that rebellion had grown under the system he built as Head-Diamond, relieved him of the role, made him the Authority's sole diplomat, and sent him away on leave.
>
> Then she addressed everyone. The age of Courts was over. Era 2B had begun: three branches of government, the Diamonds' courts turned into corporations, a meritocracy like Era 1, the old currency restored, and new gems made only from analogous pairs of essence. To take back the crown, Alpha removed her veil, opened a void in her own body, and drew out the gemstone of Omega White Diamond, which she had held since the war. She merged it into her own. When the light cleared, she was Astral Black Diamond, Head-Diamond of the Imperious Authority. Sensei, who had fought in that war, could only hope there was nothing of White left inside her.
>
> Emerald took her share of Mint's old business and renamed it Verdant Industries.

**HELIODOR · THE ALBIAN KINDERGARTEN**
> While the coronation went on, the Agency team flew to the Albian Kindergarten in Heliodor's southern hemisphere, where two Lapises had spotted a gem sneaking around the sectors. They found a gutted injector and a lab left open. Inside was a Sphene, a STORM technician, and soon after, the White Knight. They held their own, and Tulip's Pearl Plum punched the Knight in the chest, but the two took what they had come for and escaped. Cyrus called it a failure. Tulip, Plum, and both Lapises had seen too much to go home and joined the team.

**HOMEWORLD · THE RUINS OF MINT INDUSTRIES**
> What was left of Mint Industries sat at the bottom of an eighty-meter crater. Somewhere inside were four ship cores Mint had built for STORM, and the Agency sent a team down to take them first. Below the offices and labs was the Restricted Sector, where Mint had run experiments no Diamond should: a machine that forced gems to fuse whether they could or not, a vault of failed results kept in stasis, and illegal weapons. Some of the results were loose. The team fought a fusion of five gems pressed together, and Stellar recognized one of its shards as a Garnet she had known in her cargo-running days. Stellar squeezed through a vent into Mint's private lab, which held an unfinished weapon store, a core no bigger than a fist, and a fragment in stasis that took her hand off when she touched it.
>
> One of their own, a Peridot called Spot, had slipped away on her own. They found her in the Ship Core Vault with the Sphene's old powder pistol pressed to her gem. A Lapis came up behind the Sphene, a Ruby knocked the shot into the ceiling, and Stellar finished her. Then a smaller cousin of the worm from the ballroom came up through the floor and dragged its prey back underground. The mounted cores were fakes. Behind a hidden wall was Mint's decay vault, holding the real four, Alpha, Beta, Gamma, and Delta, along with notes on the machine they powered and what STORM had paid for them. Mint had never trusted STORM. Topside, Spot admitted she had been STORM herself, had left because of what they did, and had been feeding the Head Directors what she knew. Stellar, who had fired a gun to save her, expected to stand trial for it.

**VERDANT INDUSTRIES**
> Emerald Diamond came down after them to gut the place. She took the fragment, the heart of something called Project Resonator, into her own gem, and found Mint's fabricator still printing something from a material that no longer exists. She lifted the entire ruin out of the ground, sealed what was left of the experiments in new basements, and rebuilt it as a tower crowned with a spacehook for catching cargo between worlds. Not long after, she learned what one of her own projects had done: a Pearl named Ash had had her mind torn out of her gem and forced into a machine body.

**THE END OF TIMELINE IV**
> No record survives of how the end began. What is known is how it finished. A Deity Diamond can only take another's Pillar one way: she forces her own light into the other's gemstone and tears the Pillar out, and the gemstone shatters as it goes. Astral Black Diamond had to do it twice, first to Theta Gold and then to Epsilon Blue, until she held every Pillar the Deity Diamonds had left. It still wasn't enough. Universe Diamond reset the timeline, and Timeline IV was gone. It was not the first time she had done it.

**A NOTE ON UNIVERSE DIAMOND**
> In this timeline, the only gem who spoke her name in public was a Chrysocolla from a cult called the Gems of the Elder Ones, banned from forty-seven systems. On the Crystal Hour, she claimed the Diamonds were only fingers on the hand of a true deity, and that perfect gems had existed before Black Diamond. Lady Crimson shattered her gem on air and called it a bit. Nobody believed a word of it.

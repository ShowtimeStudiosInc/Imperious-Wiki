# BELOW THE SURFACE…

**RECAP: PART 1**
> A few days after Orientation Day, Cyrus waited in the District 3 alley where the cloaked figure had been seen leading creatures underground. One by one, the others showed up. Wasp was already there messing with her kunai, and Graphene arrived admitting she'd missed the whole thing the first time. Two Lapises, Flappy and Lappy, said Ms. Alina had sent them, though she'd never told Cyrus. Sapphire "Tulip" came with a quiet Rhodonite named Rhody, and an Emerald named Emmy stepped out of the shadows, having listened to everything. Cyrus told them the figure had come back up a day or two ago and hadn't returned since, and Tulip admitted it had slipped past her future vision completely.
>
> More kept coming. E-Ash clanked in, still in her old body, and Stellar Gold joined with her hand on her holster. Cyrus recognized Gold as the former Mayor of his Metal Society city, which she flatly denied. Another Metal, Dustin, wandered in late, then Morganite, apologizing and speaking far more formally than before, followed by Light Pink Agate and her new Pearl, Ciner.
> Once everyone had gathered, Cyrus warned them it would probably get dangerous, and that once they went below, they'd be a party who looked out for each other. He passed his hand over the floor, the disguise faded, and a metal hatch opened up out of the ground. One by one, they climbed down after him. Dustin spotted Catseye, who had just arrived and was trying to leave, and dragged her down with him while she yelled about her coat. E-Ash followed with a happy "Weeee!" and Morganite's Ruby guards jumped in last.
>
> The drop left them in a cold concrete landing under the street. Out in the hallway, E-Ash nearly electrocuted herself trying to steal power from a junction box, so Emmy charged her slowly through her own hands instead. Catseye climbed onto Dustin's back and refused to walk, and Dustin carried her anyway. When Gold asked what Cyrus expected to find, he said monsters, probably. At the end of the hall stood three doors. Cyrus took half the group left into the Secondary Filter Hub, and Wasp took the rest right into the Grand Canal Vein Observation.
> In the Filter Hub, a room of huge dusty air scrubbers still running after what looked like ages, Rhody stored Catseye's coat in her gem to keep it safe from chemicals, and Dustin found a hatch locked behind a keypad. E-Ash plugged her tail in to hack it, but ran into a firewall written in a programming language she'd never seen, and got locked out. Cyrus promised to get her an upgrade, then started hacking it himself. Emmy called him impressive, which flustered him so badly he had to start over.
>
> Meanwhile, Wasp's group found a window looking out over a huge pipe of glowing teal fluid. It was the same fluid that flows through the Canal across the city above, and a plate on the wall read "Coolant Artery-4236B." Stellar Gold found a map showing the fluid lines all leading to a set of huge chambers. Gold was sent to tell Cyrus, got lost immediately, and Flappy went instead.
> Cyrus finally broke through the firewall just as the two groups reunited, and found protective gear against chemicals below the hatch, which Rhody stored in her gem. Deciding the floor was safe enough, he told everyone to split up and explore. Cyrus and Emmy searched two closets. In one, among old grease-stained logs, Cyrus found a page recording when this place was built: long before the city above. They also found E-Ash stuck in a cabinet, down to 7 percent power, and pulled her out. On the way out, Emmy told Cyrus to call her Emmy, and he left blushing. Elsewhere, Flappy found strange codes stamped on the pipes, Tulip and Rhody freed a log book mentioning something called the Repair Droids, and Graphene found a giant iron door sealing off the rest of the facility.
>
> Pink and Ciner found a platform over a shaft that dropped through Homeworld's crust toward the machine levels below. Then a corrupted Aquamarine leapt from the wall and slammed Pink against the railing. Ciner screamed for help. Pink pried it off just as Cyrus arrived, and it fled through the hallway, raining spears of water down on the others. Tulip blocked hers with ice, but Maddy took a hit.
> Back in the hall, everyone shared what they'd found. Cyrus told them the facility likely dated back to the middle of Era 0, and Stellar Gold remarked it must have been from back when the Gempire wasn't enslaving them. Then Graphene loudly told Dustin that Catseye had a crush on him, to Catseye's horror, and Morganite begged everyone to stop talking about "crushing" gems. Cyrus snapped at them all to focus and asked Graphene to lead them to the door.
>
> The door couldn't be hacked, so Dustin forced the override wheel himself while Catseye and Morganite cheered him on. It swung open, and alarms began blaring. Red warnings said passing this point would send security alerts across the sector. Cyrus told everyone to get further in before the place filled with Security Droids, and the whole group ran through the door after him. Happy reminded Morganite this had been a bad idea, while Maddy called it the most exciting thing he'd felt in years, which Morganite pointed out was odd, since they'd only been made a week ago.

**PART 1 HIGHLIGHTS**
> ⟡ Cyrus gathered a large group and opened a hidden hatch in a District 3 alley, leading to a facility beneath the city.
> ⟡ Records found inside show the facility was built long before Homeworld's cities, likely in the middle of Era 0.
> ⟡ A pipe of glowing teal "coolant" runs beneath District 3, the same fluid as the city's Canal. A map shows it flowing to a set of huge chambers.
> ⟡ A locked hatch was guarded by a firewall written in a programming language no one recognized.
> ⟡ A log mentions Repair Droids that run the facility's power on their own.
> ⟡ A shaft drops through Homeworld's crust toward the machine levels below.
> ⟡ A corrupted Aquamarine attacked Pink and Maddy, and is still loose in the facility.
> ⟡ Dustin forced open the security door, setting off alarms, and the group ran deeper before security could arrive.

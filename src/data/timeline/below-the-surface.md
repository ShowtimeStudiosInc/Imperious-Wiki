# BELOW THE SURFACE…

**RECAP: PART 1**
> A few days after Orientation Day, Cyrus waited in the District 3 alley where the cloaked figure had been seen leading creatures underground. One by one, the others showed up. Wasp was already there messing with her kunai, and Graphene arrived admitting she'd missed the whole thing the first time. Two Lapises, Flappy and Lappy, said Ms. Alina had sent them, though she'd never told Cyrus. Sapphire "Tulip" came with a quiet Rhodonite named Rhody, and an Emerald named Emmy stepped out of the shadows, having listened to everything. Cyrus told them the figure had come back up a day or two ago and hadn't returned since, and Tulip admitted it had slipped past her future vision completely.
>
> More kept coming. E-Ash clanked in, still in her old body, and Stellar Gold joined with her hand on her holster. Cyrus recognized Gold as the former Mayor of his Metal Society city, which she flatly denied. Another Metal, Dustin, wandered in late, then Morganite, apologizing and speaking far more formally than before, followed by Light Pink Agate and her new Pearl, Ciner.
> Once everyone had gathered, Cyrus warned them it would probably get dangerous, and that once they went below, they'd be a party who looked out for each other. He passed his hand over the floor, the disguise faded, and a metal hatch opened up out of the ground. One by one, they climbed down after him. Dustin spotted Catseye, who had just arrived and was trying to leave, and dragged her down with him while she yelled about her coat. E-Ash followed with a happy "Weeee!" and Morganite's Ruby guards jumped in last.
>
> The drop left them in a cold concrete landing under the street. Out in the hallway, E-Ash nearly electrocuted herself trying to steal power from a junction box, so Emmy charged her slowly through her own hands instead. Catseye climbed onto Dustin's back and refused to walk, and Dustin carried her anyway. When Gold asked what Cyrus expected to find, he said monsters, probably. At the end of the hall stood three doors. Cyrus took half the group left into the Secondary Filter Hub, and Wasp took the rest right into the Grand Canal Vein Observation.
> In the Filter Hub, a room of huge dusty air scrubbers still running after what looked like ages, Rhody stored Catseye's coat in her gem to keep it safe from chemicals, and Dustin found a hatch locked behind a keypad. E-Ash plugged her tail in to hack it, but ran into a firewall written in a programming language she'd never seen, and got locked out. Cyrus promised to get her an upgrade, then started hacking it himself. Emmy called him impressive, which flustered him so badly he had to start over.
>
> Meanwhile, Wasp's group found a window looking out over a huge pipe of glowing teal fluid. It was the same fluid that flows through the Canal across the city above, and a plate on the wall read "Coolant Artery-4236B." Stellar Gold found a map showing the fluid lines all leading to a set of huge chambers. Gold was sent to tell Cyrus, got lost immediately, and Flappy went instead.
> Cyrus finally broke through the firewall just as the two groups reunited, and found protective gear against chemicals below the hatch, which Rhody stored in her gem. Deciding the floor was safe enough, he told everyone to split up and explore. Cyrus and Emmy searched two closets. In one, among old grease-stained logs, Cyrus found a page recording when this place was built: long before the city above. They also found E-Ash stuck in a cabinet, down to 7 percent power, and pulled her out. On the way out, Emmy told Cyrus to call her Emmy, and he left blushing. Elsewhere, Flappy found strange codes stamped on the pipes, Tulip and Rhody freed a log book mentioning something called the Repair Droids, and Graphene found a giant iron door sealing off the rest of the facility.
>
> Pink and Ciner found a platform over a shaft that dropped through Homeworld's crust toward the machine levels below. Then a corrupted Aquamarine leapt from the wall and slammed Pink against the railing. Ciner screamed for help. Pink pried it off just as Cyrus arrived, and it fled through the hallway, raining spears of water down on the others. Tulip blocked hers with ice, but Maddy took a hit.
> Back in the hall, everyone shared what they'd found. Cyrus told them the facility likely dated back to the middle of Era 0, and Stellar Gold remarked it must have been from back when the Gempire wasn't enslaving them. Then Graphene loudly told Dustin that Catseye had a crush on him, to Catseye's horror, and Morganite begged everyone to stop talking about "crushing" gems. Cyrus snapped at them all to focus and asked Graphene to lead them to the door.
>
> The door couldn't be hacked, so Dustin forced the override wheel himself while Catseye and Morganite cheered him on. It swung open, and alarms began blaring. Red warnings said passing this point would send security alerts across the sector. Cyrus told everyone to get further in before the place filled with Security Droids, and the whole group ran through the door after him. Happy reminded Morganite this had been a bad idea, while Maddy called it the most exciting thing he'd felt in years, which Morganite pointed out was odd, since they'd only been made a week ago.

**PART 1 HIGHLIGHTS**

> ⟡ Records found inside show the facility was built long before Homeworld's cities, likely in the middle of Era 0.
> ⟡ A pipe of glowing teal "coolant" runs beneath District 3, the same fluid as the city's Canal. A map shows it flowing to a set of huge chambers.
> ⟡ A locked hatch was guarded by a firewall written in a programming language no one recognized.
> ⟡ A log mentions Repair Droids that run the facility's power on their own.
> ⟡ A shaft drops through Homeworld's crust toward the machine levels below.
> ⟡ A corrupted Aquamarine attacked Pink and Maddy, and is still loose in the facility.
> ⟡ Dustin forced open the security door, setting off alarms, and the group ran deeper before security could arrive.

**RECAP: PART 2**
> The door slammed shut behind them, and the group ran down the stairs of another huge shaft, the same kind of drop they had seen from the Access Landing in Zone 1. Catseye screamed that she didn't want to die. Pink told her nobody does, and Graphene said she'd be surprised. At the bottom, Cyrus held a second door open until every single gem was through, then shut it behind them.
>
> It opened onto a catwalk over a giant chamber where steel pistons the size of city blocks rose and fell. Cyrus handed out the flashlights he'd collected and warned that they had a few minutes at most to find a place to hide. He took one step forward, and the next piston strike shook the whole catwalk. Half the group was thrown over the side, left hanging from the railing. Morganite and Happy pulled Ciner back up. Cyrus grabbed Emmy and sent out a drone with arms to lift Wasp, who called him an asshole and then flopped onto the walkway. Lappy caught Dustin, Pink pulled Ash up, and Catseye clung to Dustin like an actual cat. Maddy fell, and the drone caught them just in time. Morganite tackled Maddy in a crying hug. Happy joined in and didn't let go for a long time.
>
> While everyone recovered, Ciner pulled Pink aside. She said she had nearly fallen too, that she'd been no help when the Aquamarine attacked Pink, and that more of those monsters were surely waiting. Then she asked if they should fuse. Pink told her she had never been dead weight, but that she trusted her.
>
> Cyrus spotted a control room on the upper catwalks. He sent Lappy and Flappy flying up to find the off switch, and Lappy found it. The pistons stopped, and everyone ran up the stairs into the booth. Cyrus told them not to touch any buttons. Graphene had already pressed one, and the pistons started back up. Lappy turned them off again.
>
> Looking out the window, the group watched the door they'd come through burst open. Security Droids and Drones poured in and started searching the lower catwalks. Cyrus noted that the technology in this room was much older than anything above, and that the deeper they went, the more primitive it seemed to get. Ash plugged her tail into the console. This time the firewall was written in something close to the Gempire's own programming language, and she got in on her first try. She found that the room managed fluid pressure across the whole underground with no gem needed. The floor plans were stranger. This zone didn't stay under District 3. It spread under the entire planet, with power rooms everywhere and four massive engine rooms on the north, south, east, and west sides of Homeworld. Ash asked the obvious question: what if Homeworld was built to move?
>
> Tulip looked ahead with her future vision. All she got was a foggy few flashes: a laser being fired, someone falling, and tears. Cyrus said he would not let that happen, and reminded them that a foggy vision can't be fully trusted, since the river's path always splits eventually.
>
> While the others argued, Ciner offered Pink her hand, and the two fused. A blue and reddish-pink light filled the room, and Bi-Color Andean Opal stood up, looking over her new body. Cyrus checked her through his monocle and said it was an unrecorded fusion. Then he laid out the escape plan. Morganite would ride on Opal's shoulders, with Happy riding beside her, to shield everyone with bubbles, and Stellar Gold would shoot down anything that chased them. Gold loaded real bullets and pointed out that Ash was a clanker too. Cyrus said at least they were still made of light.
>
> Cyrus threw the door open, and everyone sprinted across the upper catwalks. The Drones flew up after them and opened fire. Almost everyone took hits. Cyrus slashed back with arcs of lightning and found that the machines were weak to electricity. Stellar Gold switched to electric bullets, Emmy called down a lightning barrage, Morganite threw bubble shields over the most hurt, and Graphene bit one. One by one, the Drones went down until the last one fell. At the far stairwell, a line of Droids was coming up to meet them. Cyrus drop-kicked the first one and rode it down the stairs like a sled, knocking the rest over. He hopped off at the bottom just before the pile smashed through the railing and fell off the ledge. Catseye's first concern was her hair and clothes.
>
> Back on the piston floor, Ash pointed the way to a hatch in the far wall, and Dustin forced it open. Inside was an auxiliary pump station, its back wall lined with huge pumps pulling the same teal fluid down from the rivers above. Graphene drank some of the oil, and Morganite made her stop. Emmy guessed these pumps powered all of Homeworld's rivers. Then Wasp said what she had read on a plaque back in Zone 1: it was coolant. Cyrus finally had everyone share what they'd found. Tulip handed over the logs, and Flappy gave him the strange code from the pipes. Cyrus recognized it as a serial number. He typed it into his holopad, and it built a 3D model of the full coolant network. It ran across the entire planet and all the way down to the core.
>
> Morganite asked why a planet's core would need cooling when it is supposed to be hot. Cyrus said maybe it isn't the planet's core that needs it. Ash asked whether there even is a core. Nobody had an answer, so they moved on.
>
> The next chamber was split by a wide underground river of glowing coolant, and the drawbridge across it was raised. Opal suggested just wading across. Morganite asked for a stick to check the depth first, so Stellar Gold shaped a gold javelin and tossed it to Cyrus. The river came up to half of it, and when he pulled it out, it was frozen solid. No one waded. Cyrus went into a small booth by the bridge, found a set of thermal blankets, and started the bridge lowering. The terminal warned that the hinges were worn, so it moved very slowly.
>
> While they waited, the group finally talked. Morganite told Tulip how she had ended up here. Emmy asked Opal what gem she was now and admitted she had never been part of a fusion. Ash told Dustin that her body and gem were fully machine, and that only her mind had stayed the same.
>
> Then scurrying sounds came from both sides of the river, and a swarm of twelve corrupted Aquamarines flooded in. The tight alcove froze over. Two of them pinned Cyrus against the rock, another hooked Wasp's chain to drag her toward the river, two slammed into Maddy to get at Morganite, and the rest came at everyone else. Dustin kicked two straight into the coolant. Opal blasted one with freezing snow before it could push anyone off the edge, and Rhody smacked one with Gold's javelin. Stellar Gold put a full revolver round into one, three bullets to the gem, and shattered it.
>
> Cyrus called for Emmy to send everything she had into his blades. She poured her lightning into them, and Cyrus swung two enormous slashes in a wide cone. They called it Thunderhead Requiem. Emmy's lightning barrage finished off the rest. When it was over, Cyrus gathered the gems that were still whole, bubbled them, and sent them to the Agency. Three had been shattered, all by Gold. Tulip was badly shaken that they had killed gems at all. Rhody reminded her they had been warned at the start. Cyrus was honest with them: in this line of work, it's do or die. Corrupted gems can't be reasoned with, but they should still try to reason with any gem who can think. Gold told them thinking in battle gets you killed. Happy called that terrible advice. Tulip went silent and stayed that way.
>
> The bridge settled into place, and the group crossed. Wasp told Cyrus and Emmy that if they kissed, she would throw up. Emmy pointed out that they had only just met.
>
> In the next vault, glass pipes carried the coolant further down into the planet. Cyrus said it hadn't even been a full day yet, though night was starting on the surface. Wasp and Catseye were at each other's throats by now, and Dustin had to step between them. Morganite had gone quiet, and Maddy stayed close to cheer her up.
>
> The catwalk ended in a three-way fork. Left was the Secondary Hydraulic Manifold, right was Maintenance Observation Post 2-D, and straight ahead was Engineering Checkpoint Delta. Cyrus split them up. Wasp took Ash, Morganite, Dustin, and Catseye right, and everyone else went left with Cyrus. When Wasp asked for tape for Catseye's mouth, Opal pulled some out of her Pearl gem.
>
> In the Manifold, Cyrus found a heavy wrench and had Opal store it. He said it would come in handy once they met up with someone named Marlowe. Stellar Gold found an ingot of gold and shaped it into a crown, Opal found a small sheet of scrap metal, and Tulip checked her future again. This time she saw a corrupted gem right in her face.
>
> In the Observation Post, Morganite grabbed a stack of old log sheets. Ash ate them like a ticket muncher and read them back from a holopad in her eye. The station was once used by engineering supervisors to watch over something beyond the glass, and the logs listed other facilities next to their names. Ash also found a metal pipe to use as a weapon. Catseye, still taped, kicked her off Dustin's shoulders.
>
> The two groups met back at the fork. With every side path searched, Cyrus led everyone down the middle path toward Engineering Checkpoint Delta.

**RECAP: PART 3**
> The middle path ended at Engineering Checkpoint Delta, a heavy security gate between Zone 2 and Zone 3. Its keypad flashed a red override error, and magnetic bars held the blast panels shut. Ash ran at it with her new pipe and bounced right off. This door couldn't be forced, so Cyrus attached his holopad to the keypad and started hacking. It took a near-perfect hack to get through, and Cyrus came close a few times. In the end, it was Ash who cracked it. She said the firewall had been simpler for some reason. Emmy said she thought Cyrus was the only good hacker in the group. Ash replied that the two of them clearly had each other hypnotized, and the gate slid open.
>
> Past the gate, a ladder dropped into a sealed vestibule. Its blast doors hung open on warped hinges, and scorch marks spread out from the frames, left by explosions long ago. Ash's signal was falling apart, and she warned Cyrus she would disconnect if she went any lower. Gold told her to leave, then, since all she did was get carried around. Cyrus mentioned there was supposed to be an execution on the surface that day, and Wasp realized she had missed it.
>
> Ash filled them in on the news from up top. A massive cosmic storm was sweeping the outer colonies, and it knocked out technology wherever it hit. Every colony in the outer rim was being evacuated, and something had destroyed an entire town in the middle of its evacuation. Lappy pointed out quietly that a storm like that is why the two of them were here at all. That storm was Ash's problem too. The datacenter hosting her consciousness sat out in those same outer colonies, and it was going dark. She took one step onto the ladder, lost her connection completely, and dropped like a sack of potatoes. Cyrus had Rhody store her in her gem.
>
> Wasp panicked. Her Pearl, Cream, was still up there. Morganite told her Cream had surely been evacuated. Cyrus reminded Wasp that she had left Cream at the base, and that there was no way back the way they came anyway. Emmy started worrying about her own ship and crew. Dustin flipped Catseye around to carry her in his arms, and she hid her blushing face in her hair.
>
> Beyond the vestibule, a short hall ended at another door, and this one had been welded shut from the inside. Thick beads of melted metal lined the frame, and the keypad was destroyed. Whoever had been down here in the facility's final hours had not wanted anyone to reach what was on the other side. Cyrus pulled out a glowing circular saw and started cutting a hole. Gold suggested they fuse and make a plasma torch instead, then announced she was stealing Emmy's man. Wasp offered to fuse with Emmy in front of Cyrus to get even. Cyrus said he was almost done anyway, and that he would rather save his first fusion for someone close to him. Then he kicked the cut piece in, and it fell.
>
> The room beyond was dark and damp. The only light came from a large hologram of Homeworld floating over a console in the middle. Morganite asked Rhody to light the way, and Rhody's gem lit up the room. The floor, walls, and ceiling were covered in hive resin. In the dark, pair after pair of glowing eyes opened. It was a nest.
>
> Seventeen corrupted gems came at them all at once: six Rubies, five Quartzes, two Peridots, three Agates, and a massive Jasper leading them. The Rubies swept fire across Cyrus, Emmy, and Morganite. The Quartzes body-slammed the front line. The Peridots tore metal panels off the server columns and threw them as shrapnel, and the Agates let out a roar that drove the whole nest into a frenzy. The Jasper charged straight into Opal and threw her across the floor. She got back up and froze it with a blast of snow. Gold took a hit that cost her half her health. Cyrus answered with two lightning slashes that tore through three of the Rubies. Morganite was too scared to fight back, but her fear made her harder to hurt. When a Quartz charged at Wasp, she grabbed Catseye out of Dustin's arms and used her as a shield.
>
> Then Lappy and Flappy pulled every drop of moisture out of the damp air, and Tulip added her ice. Together they raised a huge tidal wave full of ice spikes and sent it through the room. It wiped out the entire nest in one hit.
>
> Cyrus stared at the Lapises and said he was glad he wasn't on the other end of that water. He gathered the poofed gems, bubbled them, and sent them off. Gold quietly pocketed a few for herself, and Morganite asked if she was sure she should be taking those.
>
> Dustin was furious. He screamed at Wasp that she could have gotten Catseye killed. Wasp yelled back that she already had a cracked gem and wasn't risking another, and that Catseye needed to learn to fight for herself instead of complaining while Dustin doted on her. Dustin told her that throwing someone in front of a monster doesn't teach them anything, and that they were supposed to be a team. As he shouted, black smoke started pouring off his body. Wasp began coughing hard and backed away. Lappy and Flappy dampened the air around him until it settled.
>
> Cyrus told Dustin to save it for after they got out, and went to the console. With some work, the hologram of Homeworld split open and showed what was inside: rooms, engines, and machinery running all through it. Homeworld isn't just a planet with machines buried in it. The planet itself is a machine.
>
> Then red dots began appearing across the hologram, scattered all through the planet's underground, including the room they were standing in. Each was a corrupted nest. Holopads popped up beside them, all showing one date. It was the date of Alpha Black Diamond's birthday. Tulip noted that it was coming up soon. Cyrus looked horrified. "They're everywhere," he said. "Nests, everywhere." Then he told them they had to leave, now. He kicked down the nearest door and ran through it, and everyone followed. Dustin grabbed Wasp and Catseye on the way out, even after everything.
>
> The door opened onto an emergency borehole cut steeply up through the rock, with steel rungs bolted into the wall and starlight showing far above. Cyrus climbed first. As he went, he dropped a drone down to the group carrying a message: meet him at the General Docking Bay in a couple of days. One by one, the group climbed out into the freezing tundra of Sector Null. Opal said a follow-up didn't sound so bad. Stellar Gold said she was not doing that, since she had nearly died down there and gotten nothing for it. At the top, Rhody reached into her gem and pulled out Ash, along with all the gear she'd been holding. Ash was still completely shut down.

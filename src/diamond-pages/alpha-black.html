She is one of the three founders of the Gempire. With Theta Gold and Epsilon Blue, she made the first of its Gems, and the three of them are worshipped as the deities of Homeworld.

⟡Personality

Alpha Black is kind, and she treats the Gems close to her like her own children. Not many get that close, though. She keeps herself hidden behind a veil of darkness and only lifts it in her own rooms, for Gems she trusts.

With everyone else, she is quiet and hard to read. As head of the Agency, she expects the Gems who report to her to stay loyal and keep what they learn to themselves, so even old friends tend to be careful with what they say around her.

Theta Gold and Epsilon Blue are the two she respects most and sees as her equals.

⟡Purpose

Alpha Black runs the Agency, the empire’s intelligence network. Its spies, informants, and counter-intelligence teams work across every court and colony, and what they learn comes back to her.

Her job is to catch threats while they are still small, and to make sure the reports that reach the top have not been changed along the way. She also keeps the other Diamonds informed and protected, so they can rule in the open while she stays out of sight.

Very little of importance happens in the empire without her hearing about it.

⟡Weakness

Her daughters. They are the one subject that can bring out her wrath. When they are in danger, she stops being careful and can be made to act without thinking.

⟡Absolute Darkness Manipulation

Mastered

Her darkness, shaped by her Domain of Darkness and her place as Pillar of Space.

What It Does

Alpha Black can summon her aura as darkness anywhere on the same planet as her, and shape it however she likes: flat shadows, or solid shapes, objects, and creatures that can touch the world. Her shadows act as her eyes, ears, and mouth away from her body, and by bending the light around them she can make illusions. The veil that hangs from her ring is her darkness too. Outside of battle, The Tree is her most common sentence, sized to the offense, from a small one for a set term up to one whose roots never let go.

Passives

  • Suppression Aura. While Alpha Black is on the battlefield, her aura presses down on the enemy side. The enemy party’s Star Gauge only gains its start-of-round marks, the same as fighting in a dark area. This works on anyone, including the Deity Diamonds.
  • Darkness. Each spell from this ability that lands adds 1 Darkness to the battlefield, and Ekleipsis adds 2. Darkness caps at 5, and fades when the battle ends.
  • The Tree. When an enemy would go from Flickering to Poofed, they are Mangled instead.

Limits

Her darkness cannot reach past the planet she is on. Strong light works against it: each Fire or Light attack that lands on her side of the field removes 1 Darkness. And she never fights at full strength. Part of her is always occupied with something she will not explain. Stat changes: none.

⟡Absolute Mnemokinesis

Standard

Absolute Memory Manipulation, drawn from her Domain of Knowledge.

Parent Ability
Mnemokinesis
Complexity
Complex
Origin
Domain of Knowledge

What It Does

Alpha Black can draw the memories out of anyone she or her darkness touches, and keep them as holo-records. Done by her own hand, the effect is much stronger, and painful for anyone but a Diamond. She remembers every name she has ever heard, and every language she has ever heard spoken.

Passives

  • Sensory Scrying. As the mother of the Era 0 and Era 1 Gems, Alpha Black can see and hear through any of them, and speak through them, though she rarely does.
  • Records. Each time Mnemosyne or Anamnesis lands on a target, Alpha Black gains 1 Record on them, up to 3. Each Record gives that target -1 on every resist roll against her. Records last until the battle ends.

Limits

Her strongest Memory spells need Records first, so they are slow to get going. They are mind spells too, so anyone with a high INT is hard to reach. And drawing out memories needs contact. Stat changes: none.

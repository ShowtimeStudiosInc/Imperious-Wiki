Saturn Light Diamond

The sweet summer child at the bottom of the Diamond pyramid. Warm, playful, and by far the laziest of them all, he would almost always rather be at his club than at his desk, and no one in the Gempire throws a better party.

“Whatever life throws at you, duck and let it hit someone else.”

Personality

Saturn Light is the sweet summer child who stands at the bottom of the Diamond pyramid. He views the world in a playful light, almost the same way a Spinel does. He does not take things as seriously as he should, and he believes that bringing people together is the easiest way to resolve a conflict, whether that is by force or not.

He is by far the laziest Diamond, always choosing to hang out at his club instead of doing important paperwork. Saturn is also kind of naive. He is aware of the way certain Gems get treated, and he is highly against how some servant Gems get treated, but at the same time he will turn a blind eye to it, because at the end of the day, is it really a problem he has to deal with? Those Gems do not belong to him personally, so he sees no reason to bother interfering.

Saturn is not the type to easily show mercy to his court. Well, he is. He sees those in his court as his children, but if a Gem does something that goes against Saturn, or is simply really bad, the Gem will be shattered. No questions asked.

Purpose

Saturn organizes events between the other Diamonds and for the entire Gempire, oversees the production and distribution of Pearls, Spinels, and other servant Gems, and scouts out new potential planets for colonization and oversees terraformer Gems.

Organizing events and meetings is the job he takes incredibly great pride in. Some might even say no one has ever thrown a better party than Saturn Light himself. His other jobs get less of his care. He normally leaves finding suitable planets up to his Pearl, as he finds it extremely boring. He does like overseeing servants and terraformers, though.

Weakness

Himself, because one day he will be his own greatest downfall. The way he does things is not the same way everyone else does their job. As a Diamond he should be respected and disciplined, because who would want to listen to a Diamond who slacks off all day?

Ability 1

Absolute Restoration

Parent  Restoration Complexity  Complex Origin  Domain of Vitality

Saturn Light can restore anything to the way it was before it was hurt: mending light forms, bringing drained ground back to life, putting broken things back together, and keeping a tired Gem on her feet long past when she should have dropped. Outside of battle, he can even mend a Cracked gem, which few Gems in the empire can do. He prefers using it on others.

Passives

Rallying Aura

While Saturn Light is on the battlefield, his party Star Gauge gains 3 marks at the start of each round instead of 2.

Overflow

Healing past max HP becomes Overflow, up to 10. Overflow is lost first when the ally is hit, and it fades when the battle ends.

Weakness

Ability 2

Absolute Psychokinesis

Parent  Psychokinesis Complexity  Complex Origin  Domain of Force

Saturn Light can push, pull, lift, and throw anything with his mind, from a single chair to a crowd of Gems who have had too much fun. Every party he has ever thrown has ended with him moving someone out of the way.

Passive

Momentum

Each spell from this ability that lands gives Saturn 1 Momentum, up to 3. His next spell that deals damage uses all of it, adding +2 damage for each Momentum spent.

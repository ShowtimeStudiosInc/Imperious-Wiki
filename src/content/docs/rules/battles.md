<span class="ia-label">Rules · out of character</span>

A Battle is a fight between two groups, each called a party. A party can be a single gem or a whole squad. Battles usually start when one side confronts the other, though an ambush can change who moves first.

A battle ends when both parties agree to stop, when one party is defeated (poofed, bubbled, or spared), or when one party escapes.

## The battle menu

On their turn, each character picks one option.

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| <img class="ia-ico" src="../../images/statuses/fight.png" alt="" />**FIGHT** | Attack with a weapon or bare hands. How it rolls depends on the weapon type. See [Items](../items/). |
| <img class="ia-ico" src="../../images/statuses/act.png" alt="" />**ACT** | Do something that isn't an attack: talk, taunt, distract, throw something, calm someone down. Most ACTs are free. |
| **INVENTORY** | Use or equip something you carry. One item per character per round. Items never cost Star Points. |
| **SKILL / SPELL** | Use a move listed under one of your Abilities. A SKILL is something you learned. A SPELL comes from your gemstone or essence. Most cost Star Points. |
| **MERCY** | Spare, Bubble, Defend, or Protect. |

An Umbrella Ability with no SPELLs listed under it can't be used in battle yet, since the character hasn't learned any spells for it. See [Abilities](../abilities/).

## Mercy

- **Spare:** Every enemy who is ready to be spared leaves the battle peacefully, all at once.
- <img class="ia-ico" src="../../images/statuses/bubbled.png" alt="" />**Bubble:** Captures one enemy gemstone instead of letting them go. Needs a bubbling Ability or item.
- **Defend:** Brace for the enemy's turn. Until your next turn you take half damage from every hit, and you add a mark to the Star Gauge. A shield makes this stronger. See [Items](../items/#shields-and-defend).
- **Protect:** Stand over a poofed or cracked ally. Every hit aimed at them hits you instead. Protect gives no damage reduction, but it fills the gauge more than Defend does.

### The Mercy meter

Every character has a Mercy meter, from 0% up. It shows how ready they are to stop fighting. The person playing the character sets it, and it should make sense for the story. At 100% or more, the character can be spared. Corrupted gems are the main exception: calming them down is how those fights are won.

## Reactions

Once per round, a character may act during someone else's turn. This is called a Reaction. Some SKILLs, SPELLs, and ACTs are marked **[REACTION]**. Answering an ACT aimed at you with an ACT of your own also counts as your Reaction.

## Group actions

Two or more characters can spend their turns on one ACT together. Group ACTs are stronger than solo ones. Fusing mid-battle is a group action. See [Fusion](../fusion/).

## Position

Battles don't use a grid. A character is in one of three places:

- **Near:** close enough to strike.
- **Far:** out of melee reach. Only Ranged weapons can hit a target who is Far.
- **Behind:** an ally stands between them and the enemy.

Moving between them is part of your turn.

## Star Points

Star Points, or SP, are shared by the whole party and come from the Star Gauge.

- The Star Gauge holds **10 marks**. When it fills, the party gains **5 SP** and the gauge empties.
- The gauge gains **2 marks** at the start of every round.
- A character adds **1 mark** when they land a critical hit, fully dodge an attack, or Defend.
- Protect adds **2 marks**.
- The party can hold up to **9 SP** at once.

### Light and darkness

<img class="ia-ico dark" src="../../images/statuses/darkness.png" alt="" />Gems are made of light, and darkness weakens them.

- **Dark areas:** the gauge only gains its start-of-round marks.
- **Total darkness:** the gauge gains nothing at all.

Some gems, like those of Nightshade Violet's court, are not affected. Their Gem-Type Profile will say so.

## Poofing, cracking, and shattering

A gem's body is hard light held around their gemstone. When it takes more damage than it can hold, it starts to break down. Each step below follows from the one before.

1. <img class="ia-ico" src="../../images/statuses/flickering.png" alt="" />**Flickering.** At 0 HP or below, a gem's form starts to flicker. They stay in the battle and can still act. At the start of their next turn they poof, unless they are healed above 0 HP or the battle ends first. Defending while Flickering holds the form together for one more turn.
2. **Poofed.** The gem retreats into their gemstone and can't act. Their HP drops to minus half their max. Each party turn they recover 1/6 of their max HP, rounded up. Metals recover 1/8 instead, since they reform slower. Once above 0 HP, they reform with 1/8 of their max HP. Healing reforms them with whatever HP the heal brings them to.
3. <img class="ia-ico" src="../../images/statuses/cracked.png" alt="" />**Cracked.** A Flickering or Poofed gem who takes another hit is Cracked. So is a gem knocked down to minus their full max HP in one hit. Cracked gems don't recover on their own. Only Abilities that mend cracks can help.
4. **Shattered.** Any damage to a Cracked gem shatters them. Protect is the only way to stop it.

Organics collapse instead of poofing and follow the same steps. A Cracked organic is critically wounded.

### Fighting alone

A character with no party can't be poofed by a single hit. Damage that would take them to 0 HP leaves them at 1 HP instead, and they become ready to be spared right away. Hitting them again Cracks them.

## Ending early

- **Ambush:** catching the enemy off guard gives your party the first round.
- **Instant Win:** if the party far outclasses the enemy's Threat Class, staff may call the battle won right away.

## Experience

Winning a battle gives EXP, whether the enemy was poofed, bubbled, or spared. A character still poofed when the battle ends gets no EXP. See [Stats & Leveling](../stats/#level-and-exp) for how much each level needs.

## Bosses

Enemies of Threat Class Titan and above may have Boss Actions: extra moves between the party's turns, and a few chances to shake off a status. Their sheet will say how many.

<span class="ia-label in-world">In-World</span>

When two or more Gems move in sync closely enough, their forms combine into a single, larger being: a fusion. A fusion is a person in her own right, with a personality made from both of her halves, and she lasts only as long as they stay in sync.

## Why fusion is regulated

Under Imperial law, every Gem answers for what she does. A fusion makes that difficult. If a fusion breaks the law, it isn't always clear which half is responsible. Fusion also lets Gems become far more than their Gem-Type was made to be, which sits badly in an empire where every Gem's place is set before she emerges. So fusion is allowed, but only in three cases.

| Case | When it applies |
| --- | --- |
| **Emergencies** | Gems may fuse when their lives are in danger. Afterward they must report the fusion within a day. A Kyanite may be asked to confirm their account. |
| **Work Permits** | A court may issue a permit for a specific job, like heavy labor, construction, or a military operation. The permit names the Gems, the job, and how long the fusion may last. Staying fused past it, or fusing for anything else, breaks the law. |
| **Marriage** | Married Gems may fuse whenever they like. A marriage must be licensed by a Kyanite, who interviews both Gems separately. Since a Kyanite can always tell when a Gem is lying, no one can be married against her will or for false reasons. |

Fusing outside these three cases is a crime. Penalties depend on the reason and the outcome. Out in Sector Null's rogue camps, nobody checks permits, and fusions there are common.

## Same-type and cross-type

On Homeworld, fusion between Gems of the same type has always been the accepted kind. Two Rubies fusing for a job draws no attention. Fusion between different types is just as legal under the three cases, but it raises eyebrows, especially in the higher courts, where most Aristocrats would never be seen doing it. Among the Militia, nobody makes much fuss.

## Who else can fuse

- **Metals** can only fuse with other Metals of compatible alloys. Their makeup isn't compatible with a Gem's, so a Gem and a Metal can never fuse.
- **Pearls** follow the same three cases. For a work permit, her superior must agree.

## Perma-fusions

Some Gems are so compatible that they can stay fused indefinitely. This is rare, and it's almost always between Gems who are deeply in love.

A perma-fusion must be married before she can be recognized. Once she is, the law treats her as one person, with both halves' names kept on record. If she ever unfuses, both halves return to their own lives and standing.

The empire has never settled one question: what rank does a perma-fusion hold when her halves came from different ranks? Courts still argue about it, and every new case reopens the argument.

## Playing a fusion

- **Every player involved must agree out of character** before their characters fuse, and any of them can end the fusion at any time.
- **A fusion you plan to play again** needs her own sheet, submitted like any character.
- **Perma-fusions need staff approval.**
- **Permits, licenses, and reports** are part of the roleplay. You don't need staff to issue them, but getting caught without one has consequences in character.
- Fusing in the middle of a battle is a **group action**. See [Battles](../battles/#group-actions).

## Building a fusion's sheet

1. **Stats.** Add up the stats of every component gem. Work out each component's stats first, including their Modifiers.
2. **HP.** Add up each component's own HP total. Don't use the 20 + (DEF x 2) formula on the fused DEF.
3. **Abilities.** Everything from both halves carries over. Abilities that fit together merge into one parent ability on the fusion's sheet, with the spells listed under it.
4. **Fusion Ability.** A fusion may have one ability of her own, on top of what her halves bring.
5. **Threat Class.** Every fusion Gem-Type gets a Threat Class, like Standard, Elite, Champion, or Titan, so members can tell how dangerous she is. It's judged for each fusion on its own, not by a formula.

Geodes and Alloys are **not** fusions. They're Gem-Types of their own, with their own stats.

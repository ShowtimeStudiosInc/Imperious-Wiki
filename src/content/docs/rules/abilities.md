Every ability on the server has three parts: a **Rating** for how strong it is, a **Type** for the role it plays, and a **Class** for the kind of power it draws from.

## Umbrellas and spells

Most powers on a Gem-Type Profile aren't something you can cast on their own. They're an **Umbrella Ability**, like Hydrokinesis, with a list of **Spells** under it. The spells are what you actually use in a battle, and each active one has an SP cost.

A gem whose ability has no spells listed under it hasn't learned to use it in a fight yet, so it can't be used in battle. Some abilities are standalone, where the ability itself is the spell. Those carry their own SP cost.

Gem powers come from the gemstone, so their uses are called **Spells**. SKILLs are learned, so their uses are called **moves**. See [SKILLs](../skills/).

## Rating

The Rating says how strong an ability is compared to other versions of itself. On a Gem-Type Profile, a Signature Ability costs stat points from the rank's budget based on its Rating.

| Rating | Profile cost | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Limited** | 1 | A small, narrow version. For a kinesis power, it usually allows only one use: generate, shape, or control. |
| **Minor** | 2 | Still fairly weak, but with more control. A Minor kinesis might generate and control, or control and shape. |
| **Base** | 3 | The standard version, with no qualifiers. |
| **Enhanced** | 4 | Unlocks new uses the Base version doesn't have: more range, a second effect, or a new use built on the Base one. Often tied to a Perfect Cut. |
| **Advanced** | 5 | Keeps the Enhanced toolkit but pushes its output much further: bigger scale, wider range, higher numbers. Rare or specialized gems. |
| **Perfect** | 6 | The strongest version. Usually only Diamonds or one of a kind cases. From here a power often branches into a higher one, like Geokinesis into Geo-Thermokinesis. |

A **Perfect Cut** raises a Gem-Type's Signature Ability one step, for example from Base to Enhanced. A **Defective** gem keeps the same Rating but gets a twisted version of the ability. See [Modifiers](../modifiers/#cut-quality).

## Type

The Type is the role an ability or spell plays in a fight. Spell and move blocks show it as an icon.

| Type | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Offensive** | Deals damage to a target |
| **Defensive** | Protects, blocks, or reduces incoming damage |
| **Buffing** | Boosts a stat or ability for yourself or an ally |
| **Debuffing** | Weakens or hinders an enemy |
| **Mobility** | Changes movement, speed, or position |
| **Utility** | No combat role: sensing, crafting, storage, communication, and the like |

## Class

The Class is the kind of power an ability draws from.

| Class | Draws from |
| --- | --- |
| **Physical** | The body itself: strength, reflexes, toughness |
| **Summoning** | Creating a physical thing, like a weapon or a material |
| **Visions** | Reaching other planes of reality (the future, the past, another dimension) or another mind |
| **Shapeshifting** | Changing your own body, either into another form or into a stronger one |
| **Enchantments** | Changing how another subject works, often called casting spells |
| **Alteration** | Manipulating matter physically |
| **Elements** | Making and controlling a type of elemental matter. A large category that grows out of Alteration and Summoning. |
| **Entropy** | Rare, chaotic, or unpredictable powers that fit nowhere else |

## Abilities on a Gem-Type Profile

| Field | What it is | Stat cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Signature Ability** | The power a Gem-Type is born with, if any | Its Rating, from the rank's budget |
| **Latent Ability** | A power the Gem-Type may unlock through story or training. Many gems never find theirs. | None |
| **ACT Bonus** | An ACT the Gem-Type is especially good at. Required when there's no Signature Ability. | None |
| **Fusion Ability** | One ability a fusion has of its own, on top of everything its halves bring | None |

Not every Gem-Type gets a power. The test is simple: **is the power the job?** A Lapis moves water because terraforming needs it. A Bismuth builds through skill, so she has no power and gets an ACT Bonus instead.

### SP on a profile

Active abilities and spells list an SP cost. Passive abilities are always on, so they're marked **PASSIVE** and cost nothing. Spell costs follow the same pricing as SKILL moves. The [SP Cost Checker](../skills/#sp-cost-checker) adds it up for you.

### Naming a power

A power's name doesn't have to come from the Superpower Wiki. Its description and mechanics are written fresh for this server. Before a new ability is finalized, it's checked against the existing [Gem-Types](../../gem-types/) so it doesn't copy another's name or effect.

## Abilities on a character

A character sheet lists:

- **Signature Abilities** copied from the Gem-Type Profile, with any spells the character has added under them.
- **Modifier Special** from their Era, Sole Essence, or Analogous Mix. See [Modifiers](../modifiers/).
- **Learned Abilities**, which are [SKILLs](../skills/), each with the character's Expertise in it: Basic, Standard, Advanced, or Mastered.
- **Latent Abilities**, only the ones the character has unlocked.

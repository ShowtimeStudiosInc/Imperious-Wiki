This wiki is the one place where every rule, Gem-Type, and piece of lore lives. Discord posts link here instead of holding the full text.

## Where to start

- **[Gem-Types](../../gem-types/)** lists every type in the empire. Pick one, read its Role and Abilities, and press **Copy Character Template** on its page to start a sheet with the stats filled in.
- **[Rules](../../rules/overview/)** covers battles, stats, items, statuses, abilities, SKILLs, modifiers, fusion, and every template.
- **Lore** is being moved over from Discord section by section. Until a section is here, the Discord post is still the source.

:::note
Stats are out-of-character mechanics. Gems can't see them in-world. See Nugget #13.
:::

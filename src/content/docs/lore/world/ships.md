<span class="ia-label in-world">In-World</span>

The empire's ships, who flies them, and what they're for. Any gem in any court can use these, but each one is most often found with the court or Gem-Type listed. The one exception is the Infiltrator Major, a United Frontier ship that the Gempire only ever sees at the Sagittarius Dwarf Station.

## Roaming Eye

| Affiliation | Crew | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Any court, most often Court Maroon | [Rubies](../../../gem-types/ruby/) | Transport |

The Roaming Eye is a small transport ship, usually crewed by a squad of Rubies. It moves troops, prisoners, and loyal gems across the empire. It isn't fast or well armed, but it's cheap to build and easy to fly, which is why there are so many of them. Most Rubies learn to pilot one before they learn anything else technical.

## Warden Ship

| Affiliation | Crew | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Any court, most often run by Emeralds | An [Emerald](../../../gem-types/emerald/) in command, [Nephrites](../../../gem-types/nephrite/) at the controls, sometimes a [Fluorite](../../../gem-types/fluorite/) | Transport and light combat |

The Warden Ship is a transport with basic weapons, built for an Emerald to command. It's the ship an Emerald takes out on scouting runs with a small crew, and the one she leads from when a fleet forms up. The Nephrites fly it, and when there's fighting ahead, a Fluorite is often on board to plan it.

## Bounty Skiff

| Affiliation | Crew | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Any court, most often Court Epsilon | An [Aquamarine](../../../gem-types/aquamarine/), with [Topazes](../../../gem-types/topaz/) on board | Transport and capture |

The Bounty Skiff is a fast, light ship built for Aquamarines on the hunt. It carries the Aquamarine, a few Topazes, and room for whatever she's been sent to bring back, whether that's an item or a gem. When she isn't on a job, it's usually docked at the Canis Major station.

## Infiltrator Major

| Era | Affiliation | Where it's seen in the Gempire | Look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Era 0 | [United Frontier](../../factions/united-frontier/), open to any citizen | Only the Sagittarius Dwarf Station | Black and white |

To the United Frontier, the Infiltrator Major is nothing special. It's a basic model that any UF Gem can use, and citizens get around in them about the way people get around in cars. To the Gempire it's another story. Its tech is well beyond anything the courts fly, it isn't in any Gempire ship registry, and the only place it's ever seen on this side is the docking bay of the Sagittarius Dwarf Station, where several are kept. Gems who arrive at the station for the first time usually don't recognize it. The station is also home to the [Era 0 Hematites](../../../gem-types/hematite-era-0/) who run the empire's news.

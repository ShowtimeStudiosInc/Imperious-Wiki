Not everything with a gemstone serves the empire. Crystal wildlife grows on its own across the colonies, and most of it would rather be left alone. Each entry lists where it lives, how big it gets, and its Threat Class.

When wildlife gets in the way of a colony, the job goes to a [Crocoite](../../../gem-types/crocoite/), who tracks the animal down, and an [Era 0-1 Steel](../../../gem-types/steel-era-0-1/), who moves it.

## Crystal Crab

| Habitat | Size | Threat Class |
|---|---|---|
| Lagoons and humid planets | Mid to large | Standard |

Crystal Crabs are large crab-like creatures covered in armored geometric spikes. Their colors come from wherever they live, so a crab from a coral lagoon looks nothing like one from a gray shoreline. Under their bellies is a drill spike, which they use to bore into solid stone and hollow out networks of nests. A colony with Crystal Crabs nearby often finds its foundations full of tunnels.

**Interactions:** Crystal Crabs leave gems alone unless they're provoked. Their spikes are sharp enough to poof a gem on the spot if one is handled roughly, and gems who try to ride them learn this fast. When a nest is dug too close to a colony, a Crocoite tracks it down and an Era 0-1 Steel moves it.

## Crystal Crocodile

| Habitat | Size | Threat Class |
|---|---|---|
| Colonial waterways and murky water | Massive | Elite (Champion while guarding young) |

Crystal Crocodiles are huge, territorial reptiles covered in crystal scales. Their scales shift to match the water they live in, which makes them almost impossible to spot until they move. They hide in murky water and ambush anything that comes too close, and many of the waterways the empire depends on already have one living in them.

**Interactions:** A Crystal Crocodile is aggressive at the best of times and deadly when guarding its young. Its teeth and spiked hide can poof a gem in one bite, and a gem who walks into its territory unprepared risks being shattered. Most colonies would rather build around a crocodile's stretch of water than try to move it. When one really has to go, it takes a Crocoite to find it and an Era 0-1 Steel to move it.

## Crystal Shrimp

| Habitat | Size | Threat Class |
|---|---|---|
| Abandoned colonies and outposts | Small | Minion |

Crystal Shrimp are small, spiked crustaceans that infest colonies after gems leave. They feed on organic debris left behind, and every segment of their bodies is covered in raw crystal spikes. They come in reds, purples, blues, and greens. One shrimp is a nuisance. A colony that's been empty long enough can have thousands.

**Interactions:** Crystal Shrimp are pests, and they're more dangerous than they look. Their spikes can poof a gem who grabs one carelessly, so handling them takes training and gloves. Crews sent to reopen an abandoned outpost usually spend their first day clearing shrimp.

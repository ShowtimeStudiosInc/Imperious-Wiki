<span class="ia-label in-world">In-World</span>

Club Imperious was built by Saturn Light Diamond in his own district, District 4 of Homeworld, to give Gems a place to rest from their work and keep the empire's spirits up. It's the best-known club in the empire, and on a busy night, Gems from every rank and court are under the same roof.

## The Door Rule

Saturn Light set one rule when the club opened, and it's carved above the entrance: rank stays at the door. Inside, an Aristocrat and a Quartz soldier are two guests like any others. Nobody may be ordered around, and nobody may demand to be served first.

The rule has limits. Imperial law still applies inside, and a Pearl's duty to report a crime doesn't stop at the door. Most guests forget that until they need to remember it.

## Inside the Club

- **The Main Floor:** The dance floor and the heart of the club. Performances run here every night.
- **The Stage:** Where Entertainment Gems perform. A good night on this stage has started more than one career.
- **The Bar:** Serves light-based drinks from across the empire. The bartenders hear everything, and are known for repeating none of it.
- **The Booths:** Private rooms that can be booked for the night. A lot of business that officially never happened was settled in a booth.
- **The Upper Lounge:** Members and invited guests only. The Door Rule still applies up here, but the guest list makes it a different place.

## The Last Dance

Close dancing brings Gems near to sync, so the dance floor clears at a set hour every night, to keep accidental fusions off it. Married Gems may stay on after that. Everyone else goes home, or to the bar.

## Who Works There

- **The Manager:** Runs the club day to day, and answers for anything that goes wrong.
- **Entertainment Gems:** Singers, dancers, and musicians, many of whom came to Homeworld hoping to be seen here.
- **Bartenders:** Serve drinks, keep secrets, and know which guests shouldn't be served another.
- **Door Staff:** Check who comes in and enforce the Door Rule. They have turned away Aristocrats who expected to skip the line.

## Busy Nights

The club is busiest on the night of the Season of the Blooming, and after every Grand Ball, when guests who spent the evening on their best behavior come here to stop.

## Playing at the Club

<span class="ia-label">Rules · out of character</span>

- Anyone can visit the Main Floor, the Stage, and the Bar.
- The Upper Lounge and private booths need an invitation or membership in character.
- Players can work at the club as Entertainment Gems, bartenders, or door staff. Running it as manager needs staff approval.

<span class="ia-label in-world">In-World</span>

The first Grand Ball was held by Theta Gold Diamond at the end of the first Festival of the First Light. Since then, balls have become the most important social events in the empire. Any Gem of Aristocratic rank or higher may host one. Alliances are made at balls, reputations are ruined at them, and more court business happens on the ballroom floor than in any meeting room.

## Who Attends

Every ball has a **host**, the Gem who arranges it, and **guests**, invited by court. Any guest below the Diamonds is called a lesser Gem for the evening, whatever her rank. Pearls attend beside their superiors, with their Reef marks on show.

Every Standard Ball keeps one throne empty, for Alpha Black Diamond. She has not attended a ball in living memory. The throne is set out anyway.

## Stage 01: Assembly

- All lesser Gems report to the ballroom when the ball is announced.
- Guests may mingle freely until the host arrives. This is when most of the real business of the night happens.
- When the host arrives, guests line up by court, then by Gem-Type.

## Stage 02: Presentation

- Each group formally introduces itself to the host.
- The Diamonds' Pearls announce their Diamonds' arrival.
- Each Diamond acknowledges the host and praises the arrangements. What a Diamond says here is repeated for centuries. A Diamond who says nothing at all can end a host's standing.
- The Diamonds take their thrones.

## Stage 03: Performance

- Lesser Gems perform synchronized court dances.
- The Diamonds watch from their thrones, and may talk among themselves.
- Court dances are designed to bring Gems close to sync without ever reaching it. A pair who slip too far and fuse on the floor have broken the law in front of every court in attendance.

## Stage 04: Conclusion

- The Diamonds descend from their thrones.
- Each Diamond chooses one guest as their partner for the closing dance. It is the highest honor a ball can give, and it has made careers. No guest has ever refused.
- The ball ends when the Diamonds' dance is over.

**Breach of Protocol will be addressed by court Agates.** Common breaches include speaking to a Diamond before they have spoken to you, leaving before the closing dance, and fusing during the Performance.

## Playing a Ball

<span class="ia-label">Rules · out of character</span>

- Official balls are announced by staff. Players of Aristocratic rank or higher may host their own, with staff approval.
- Diamond players choose their own closing dance partners. When a Diamond is played by staff, staff choose.
- Accidental fusions during the Performance need every involved player's agreement out of character first, like any fusion.

<span class="ia-label in-world">In-World</span>

A Title is a proper name, given to a Gem who has done something the empire will remember. Once granted, it replaces her designation for good. A Ruby known by her Facet and Cut becomes, from that day on, Scarlet.

## Worth Remembering

A Gem may be Titled for:

- An act of great courage or sacrifice in service to the empire
- A discovery or invention that changes how Gems live
- A central part in a major event in Imperial history
- An ability or achievement far beyond what her Gem-Type was made for
- Surviving something that should have shattered her

## Never Ask

Only the Diamonds can grant a Title, and every Diamond present must agree. No Gem may ask for a Title for herself. Asking is the surest way never to receive one.

## The Twenty-Third Floor

A Title is granted in the Imperious Authority Meeting Room, on the 23rd floor of the Throne of Homeworld. The Gem stands before the Diamonds, her deeds are read into the record, and she is given her name, usually drawn from what she did or how she did it. Her old designation is kept on file, and never used again.

## What a Name Carries

- A Title becomes a Gem's name in every official record.
- Every Gem must address her by it.
- It survives poofing, reassignment, and demotion.
- It can only be taken away if every Diamond agrees, and that has almost never happened.

## Beyond the Pearl

A Titled Gem is no longer seen as one of her Gem-Type, but as someone in her own right. In standing, a Title ranks above earning a Pearl, and for most Gems who want to rise, it's the next thing to reach for.

## Gems Who Were Never Numbered

Some Gems have names without ever being Titled. Era 0 Gems were never given designations at all. They were made by hand, no two alike, and named themselves, as the Gems of the United Frontier still do today. Metals from the Metal Society use real names too. Since Era 1, Titles have been the only way for any other Gem to earn a name, and they have grown rarer with every Era.

Every Cubic Zirconia is the one exception: they're given the Title of Grand Regent the moment they emerge, without having to earn it.

## Stories Worth a Name

- A Gem who did something worthy of a Title, and has to decide whether anyone should find out
- A newly Titled Gem whose old crew still calls her by her designation
- A Titled Gem caught in a scandal, and the Diamonds who can't agree to take her name away
- Two Gems up for a Title for the same event

## No Title on Arrival

<span class="ia-label">Rules · out of character</span>

Titles are granted by staff, through roleplay. A Gem can't be submitted with a Title she hasn't earned on the server, unless she's from Era 0 or the Metal Society.

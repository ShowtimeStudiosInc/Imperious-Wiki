<span class="ia-label in-world">In-World</span>

A Civil War is how Diamonds settle a dispute they can't resolve any other way. A real war between Diamonds could tear the empire apart, so instead, the two sides meet on a barren planet with a small, fixed force each, and fight under rules everyone has agreed to. Whoever wins decides the matter.

## Grounds for War

- A decision needs every Diamond's approval, and they can't reach it
- One Diamond's actions go directly against Imperial policy
- A dispute over territory or resources can't be settled by negotiation
- A Diamond has a serious grievance against another

Epsilon Blue Diamond rules on whether a grievance is serious enough. Most requests never reach the field.

## Sixteen on Each Side

Each side fields sixteen pieces, set out like a game of chess:

- **King:** The Diamond themself
- **Queen:** 1 High Authoritative Gem, the Field Commander
- **Officers:** 2 Median Authoritative Gems, 2 High Militia Gems, and 2 Median Militia Gems
- **Pawns:** 8 Low Militia Gems

Being chosen as a piece is an honor, even as a Pawn. A Gem who served well in a Civil War is known for it, whichever side won.

## Seventy-Two Hours

Civil Wars are fought on stage planets, barren worlds with nothing on them worth protecting. Each side gets 72 hours to build a keep before the fighting begins. The Diamond stays inside their keep for the entire war.

## Fair Play

- Building with any materials brought to the planet
- Close combat and personal weapons
- Defenses, fortifications, and traps
- Poofing enemy Gems. A poofed piece is out of the war.

## Foul Play

- Orbital bombardment, or any weapon fired from space
- Long-range weapons built to destroy keeps
- Bringing in new resources once the war has begun
- Shattering any Gem
- Attacking the other Diamond directly
- Any help from Gems outside the two forces

## When the Keep Falls

A Civil War ends when one side's keep is destroyed. The winner's decision must be carried out, and the losing Diamond may never call another Civil War over the same issue.

Court Agates watch every Civil War from start to finish. Breaking a rule can cost a side the war, and a Diamond caught cheating loses far more than the dispute.

## Betting Is Discouraged

Every Civil War is broadcast across the empire. Official Hematite commentators explain every move. Betting is officially discouraged, and practiced by nearly everyone.

## Stories From the Board

- A Low Militia Gem chosen as a Pawn, the most important thing that has ever happened to her
- A Field Commander whose Diamond's future rests on her plan
- An Agate who catches a rule being broken, by the side she secretly supports
- A Hematite commentator who has to stay neutral on air
- A Gem who bet everything on the losing side

## Joining a Civil War

<span class="ia-label">Rules · out of character</span>

Civil Wars are staff-run events. Players can apply to be pieces for a Diamond, and every fight uses the normal battle rules.

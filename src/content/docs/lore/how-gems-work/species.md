There are many species across this galaxy cluster... However, among all those species, few are intelligent... And even fewer have gotten to a stage where they have civilization... Amongst all these species, only one has reached the stage where they are capable of conquering galaxies...

Let us come together and talk of the species which inhabit our galaxy cluster.

## Gems

Gems are living gemstones that project a body made of hard light. A Gem's gemstone is who they really are. The body can be damaged and rebuilt, but the gemstone cannot be replaced.

When a Gem's body takes too much damage, it **poofs**, and the Gem retreats into their gemstone until they can reform. If the gemstone itself is broken, the Gem is **shattered**. Shattering is the only true death for a Gem, and only Diamond essence can undo it.

Gems have no need to eat, sleep, or breathe. Every Gem can shapeshift to some degree, and how well depends on their Era. Each Gem-Type was made for a purpose, such as Quartzes for the military, Pearls for service, and Sapphires for foresight, and a Gem's place in society is set by their Gem-Type before they emerge.

Most Gems emerge with a feminine form. A masculine form can happen, but it's much rarer, a bit like a recessive trait.

### Eras

- **Era 0:** The first Gems, made by hand by Alpha Black Diamond
- **Era 0 Side-B:** Omega White Diamond's Gems, from before the war
- **Era 1A:** The first Gems of the Gempire
- **Era 1B:** Gems made after Epsilon Blue Diamond emerged
- **Era 2A:** The newest Gems

### Fusion

Two or more Gems can fuse into a single, stronger being. On Homeworld, fusion has traditionally been kept to Gems of the same type, but Gems of different types can fuse. The full rules are on the [Fusion](../../../rules/fusion/) page.

## Metals

Metals are a sister species to Gems. Their bodies are denser and heavier, which makes them much harder to damage, but they take longer to reform after poofing and can only shapeshift a little. Some can carry electricity through their bodies, and others give off magnetic fields. Metals can only fuse with other Metals of compatible alloys.

Gems and Metals are closely related, but their makeup isn't compatible, in the same way two related animal species can't have young together. No Gem and Metal have ever fused, and as far as anyone knows, none ever will. A Metal is about as likely to come out masculine as feminine.

There are two kinds of Metal, and they come from very different places.

### Era 0-1: The Metal Society

The original Metals were forged in the foundries of the Metal Society, a civilization of their own that existed long before the Gempire. They go by real names rather than designations. The Metal Society only joined the Gempire in Era 2A, so even the oldest of them have been part of the empire for just a few months.

### Era 2A: Variant Metals

Since Era 2A, the Gempire has made its own Metals in kindergartens, by injecting a mix of alloys instead of the usual minerals. These are called Variant Metals, and they are made in large numbers.

### Types of Metal

- **Precious Metals:** Gold, Silver, Platinum. Administration and diplomacy.
- **Industrial Metals:** Steel, Iron, Tungsten, Copper. Heavy labor, terraforming, and electrical work.
- **Alloy Metals:** Bronze, Brass. Specialized technical work.

Most Metals serve as the empire's workforce, and their society values work done together over personal standing.

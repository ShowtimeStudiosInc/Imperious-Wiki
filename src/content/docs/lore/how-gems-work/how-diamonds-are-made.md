<span class="ia-label in-world">In-World</span>

Making a Diamond takes some very interesting procedures. The four Deity Diamonds, Alpha Black, Omega White, Theta Gold, and Epsilon Blue, were never made this way. They emerged on their own, and most Gems believe they came into being alongside the universe itself. Every Diamond since has been made like this:

1. **Choose a planet** whose materials or scenery suit the kind of Diamond being made, in looks or in role.
2. **Fill a Super-Sized Injector** with Diamond Essence and inject it into the whole planet. It takes the essence of one or two Diamonds to fill one.
3. **Extract the core.** The essence turns the planet's core into a liquid **Paragon Core**.
4. **Inject the Paragon Core** far away from any other construction or Gem sites. How much of it is used decides what emerges:
   - **All of it:** a higher Diamond
   - **90%:** a median Diamond, plus 1 Cubic Zirconia from the rest, injected right beside it
   - **80%:** a median Diamond, plus 2 Cubic Zirconias from the rest, injected right beside it
5. **Let them cook** until they emerge, leaving a huge crater on the planet.

## In Short

A Paragon Core is a completely different material from both the planet's old core and the essence used to make it. So a new Diamond has no relation to the Diamonds whose essence filled the injector. A Diamond and their Cubic Zirconias, however, are related. They emerge as siblings, and are co-dependent from birth.

A Diamond's only Cubic Zirconia stands one foot shorter than them. If there are two, each one stands half their Diamond's height. Meet the Diamonds and their Grand Regents on [Know Your Diamond](../../../diamonds/).

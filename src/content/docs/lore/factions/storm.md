S.T.O.R.M. is the biggest Rebel group in the Gempire, and most Gems will never knowingly meet one of its members. Usually the only way anyone knows they were there is the mark they leave scratched into the walls.

This page covers what being in S.T.O.R.M. is actually like, and what it's like to deal with them, for anyone who wants to play either side. For where they came from, see [Rogues & Rebels](../rogues-and-rebels/).

## Cells and Handlers

S.T.O.R.M. is broken up into small groups called cells, usually somewhere between three and ten Rebels. A cell only knows its own members, its own hideout, and one Gem above them, their Handler.

Every order a cell gets comes from their Handler, and the Handler gets their orders from someone they've never met. At the very top is the Council. No normal member has ever seen them, and nobody really knows how many of them there are.

This is a big reason the Gempire has never been able to get rid of them. Catch a Rebel, and they can only give up a few names and a hideout that's usually empty by the time anyone shows up.

It also means members have no way of checking what they're told. If a Handler says a job is for the cause, they just have to take their word for it.

## Life in a Cell

Most Rebels joined because they had nowhere else to go. A lot of them are old Gems who lost everything when the new order came in. Others are Off-Colors and Defectives who were going to be shattered, Rogues who got tired of being alone, or soldiers who walked off their posts.

In a cell, they get things the Gempire never gave them, like being called by a name instead of a code, getting a say in what the cell does, and knowing someone will come for them if they're caught.

Most of the actual work is small. Raiding supply runs, stealing tech, wrecking ships, sneaking Gems off planets before they get shattered, and embarrassing the elite whenever they get the chance. To most members it feels like the right thing to do, and a lot of the time it is.

Every cell follows two rules. Don't ask about anything above your Handler, and don't leave. Gems who leave S.T.O.R.M. usually aren't seen again.

## Going Up Against Them

For the Gempire, S.T.O.R.M. isn't something you can just fight your way out of. Raid a hideout and you catch a few Rebels who don't know anything. Shatter them and the cell next door gets angrier and bigger.

Rebels don't fight fair either. They go after places nobody's guarding, they're gone before help shows up, and they have no problem wearing a Gempire uniform to get inside. The colonies that think they're safe are usually the ones that get hit.

The worst part is that none of the small jobs are really small. One cell steals a part. Another cell in a different galaxy steals another one. A third gets told to clear out some old station nobody uses. None of them know about each other, so none of them can see what it's all adding up to.

Only the Council knows that.

<span class="ia-label in-world">In-World</span>

The Agency is an intelligence organization serving both the Gempire and the United Frontier. It works alongside the courts rather than under any one of them, and its agents are drawn from both sides of the border, chosen for their skills rather than their caste.

It handles the work no single court is built to manage alone: field missions, spying, long-term record keeping, specialized gear, and field medicine for agents on dangerous assignments. The courts look after their own territory and their own Gems. The Agency watches the threats that cross those lines.

It started small. During Era 1A, Alpha Black Diamond quietly set up an intelligence network to track anything unusual across the empire, and gave it to Hester, an Era 0 Ruby, to run. In Era 1B, when the first signs of S.T.O.R.M. appeared, Alpha decided a network wasn't enough, and built it into the Agency, open to the United Frontier as much as the Gempire.

Alpha Black Diamond holds the title of Director-General. Day to day, the Agency is run by its five Council Members, each heading one division.

## Five Seats on the Council

### HUNTER

Field operatives who carry out missions.

Council Member-01 → Head Directors → **Directors** (lead multiple teams and hand down assignments) → **Team Captains** (lead a single team) → **Operatives** (sent out to deal with problems)

### INTEL

Spies, surveillance, codebreaking, and information gathering.

Council Member-02 → **Chief Analysts** (oversee the whole intelligence picture) → **Section Handlers** (run specific regions or targets) → **Case Officers** (manage individual assignments or assets) → **Assets** (informants and agents placed in the field)

### ARCHIVE

Records and knowledge keeping.

Council Member-03, Lady Seraphine → Head Archivist → **Archivists** (oversee entire collections or subjects) → **Curators** (maintain and organize specific archives) → **Registrars** (log, catalog, and track incoming material)

### FORGE

Engineering, technology, and gear.

Council Member-04 → Chief Engineer → **Lead Engineers** (design and oversee major projects) → **Technicians** (build and maintain equipment) → **Fabricators** (handle raw production and repairs)

### WARD

Medicine, healing, and field care.

Council Member-05 → Chief Physician → **Attending Physicians** (run specific wings or units) → **Field Medics** (deployed with HUNTER teams and other divisions) → **Aides** (support staff and basic care)

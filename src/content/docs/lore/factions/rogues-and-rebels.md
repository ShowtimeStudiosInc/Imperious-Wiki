<span class="ia-label in-world">In-World</span>

Not every Gem who lives outside the empire is its enemy. Like Off-Colors and Defectives, Rogues and Rebels are often confused, and the difference matters.

A Rogue is a Gem who walked away from the Gempire to live her own life. Off-Colors and Defectives who fear what the empire would do with them, Pearls who sided with a superior against the law, and old Gems who no longer recognize the empire they served all end up as Rogues. Most want nothing more than to be left alone. Leaving one's post is still a crime, but the empire rarely chases Rogues who keep their heads down. On Census Day, every one of them disappears.

On Homeworld, most end up in Sector Null, out past the city's sky. Some shelter at Drift, a hidden camp in an ice canyon. Others make it to the Rogue Settlement out on the plains, which for many is the first place that has ever felt like home.

A Rogue becomes a Rebel the moment she acts against the Imperious Authority. That line is thinner than most Rogues think, and someone is always waiting on the other side of it to recruit them.

The Rogues' own Gem-Types are in the [Gem-Types catalog](../../../gem-types/tier/rogues/).

## The Angriest Ones

S.T.O.R.M. is the largest and most organized Rebel group in existence. Its first signs appeared during Era 1B: a Jasper who left her post and was never found, supplies missing between worlds, and the same unsigned mark scratched into walls on colonies in two different galaxies. For a long time, it stayed small.

Then the Diamonds put the new order in place, and many of the freedoms Gems had held since Era 1A were taken away. The oldest Gems, who remembered owning things, earning things, and choosing their own work, were the angriest, and many of them joined. S.T.O.R.M. has been growing ever since, absorbing smaller Rebel groups by agreement or by force.

Most of its members believe in the cause: freeing the Gempire from its rulers. Its leadership is another matter.

Whatever the top of S.T.O.R.M. actually wants, it isn't quite what they tell everyone else. More on [S.T.O.R.M.](../storm/)

## Who Hunts Them

The Agency's HUNTER division tracks Rebels across the empire. Rogues are usually left alone, unless they cross the line, or are seen talking to someone who already has.

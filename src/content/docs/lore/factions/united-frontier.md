<span class="ia-label in-world">In-World</span>

The United Frontier is the oldest society Gems have ever managed to build, and they've had plenty of practice. It started back in Era 0 as the Frontier, a handful of settlements the very first Gems put together themselves, and building things from scratch has been something of a national hobby ever since. When they packed up and moved to Hades 9, they rebuilt the lot, and they're still at it today, one colony at a time. They brought most of Era 0's technology along too, and they haven't stopped tinkering with it.

Hades 9 is split into republics, each with its own provinces and cities, and the whole Frontier votes for the High Council that runs it. Whether the Council actually runs it or just argues about running it depends on who you ask.

The Frontier's Gem-Types are in the [Gem-Types catalog](../../../gem-types/tier/united-frontier/).

## Who Shows Up to Vote

Broadly speaking, the Frontier has two kinds of Gems. There are the Era 0 Gems, who built the place, fought the White Devil's army, and will happily tell you all about it for several hours. Then there's everyone born in the Frontier since, who grew up safe on Hades 9 and have heard those stories more times than they'd like.

Nearly every argument in the Frontier comes down to the two of them. The Era 0 Gems would like things kept the way they built them, thank you very much. The younger lot would like to change a few things, and there are more of them every year. Council elections tend to be won by whichever side can be bothered to show up.

## Waiting for a Child

Frontier Gems emerge as infants and take about twenty years to reach full height. Gems who want to raise one put their names down on a list, and then they wait. And wait. Some wait for years, so finally getting a child is about the biggest day a Frontier Gem will ever have.

Nobody gets to pick the child's Gem-Type. That's left to a roulette wheel, and parents find out what they've got on the day the child emerges. Most families love the surprise. Some children spend a good few years wishing the wheel had been a bit more considerate.

Parents name their child, and once the child is old enough, they pick their own school and their own work. Some Gems end up doing exactly what their type is known for. Plenty don't, and nobody minds terribly.

## Staying Hidden

Every Frontier colony sits in a fold of space that can't be found by normal means. That's kept the Frontier perfectly safe for a very long time, and also rather small. Every new colony has to be hidden as well, which makes expanding slow, fiddly, and eye-wateringly expensive.

The oldest debate in the Council is whether to stay hidden forever. The Era 0 Gems are firmly in favour, having already been through one war and not fancying another. The younger Gems would quite like to know how long they're meant to hide from a war they weren't even around for. It's been on the Council's agenda for longer than most of them have been alive.

Every Gem-Type's standard height, lined up by rank, tallest first. Tap a bar to open that Gem-Type.

A Cubic Zirconia's height depends on their Diamond's. A Diamond's only Cubic Zirconia stands one foot shorter than them. If there are two, each one stands half their Diamond's height. The Diamonds themselves tower over everything here, so they're on [Know Your Diamond](../../diamonds/) instead.

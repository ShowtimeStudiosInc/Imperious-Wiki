# CUBIC ZIRCONIA
**``BASE STATS``**
_``HP:``_ 44
_``ATK:``_ 11
_``DEF:``_ 12
_``SPD:``_ 4
_``INT:``_ 7
_``MAG:``_ 5
_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Sub-Domain` - Cubic Zirconia carries a fragment of her Diamond sibling's essence. It gives her a lesser version of that Diamond's powers, or her own set of abilities drawn from a Sub-Domain of her Diamond's Domains. The umbrella has no SP cost. Each move built under it does, and is set on her own sheet.

``Cubic Zirconias are Diamond-like transcendent gems, created as regional managers who oversee specific territories within the Empire. Though not true Diamonds, they carry a fragment of their sibling's essence, giving them powers that resemble, but never fully match, the abilities of the Diamond they emerged alongside. Their authority stays local, making sure each region stays loyal, efficient, and lined up with the bigger goals of their ruling Diamond. Diamonds and their Zirconias share a sibling bond. When not working, they can usually be found at Club Imperious or other high ranking locations.``


# DIAMOND PEARL [SUB-TYPE OF PEARL]
**``BASE STATS``**
_``HP:``_ 34
_``ATK:``_ 5
_``DEF:``_ 7
_``SPD:``_ 5
_``INT:``_ 8
_``MAG:``_ 8
_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Hammerspace [PASSIVE]` - A Diamond Pearl can tuck items into a pocket space inside her gemstone and pull them back out. Equipping an item from it doesn't use her turn.
`Hologram Projection [1 SP]` - A Diamond Pearl makes a harmless image of light appear. One enemy who doesn't see through it attacks the image instead of its real target.
`Fragment` - A Diamond Pearl carries the lowest ranked ability under her Diamond's Domain, in a limited form. It has one move, set on her own sheet.

``Diamond Pearls are the highest ranking Pearl sub-type, placed above all other Pearls thanks to their direct service to the Diamonds themselves. Their standing puts them just below Cubic Zirconias in the hierarchy, making them both attendants and symbols of the Imperious Authority. Unlike standard Pearls, Diamond Pearls wear a crown-like crest on their head that marks their status.``

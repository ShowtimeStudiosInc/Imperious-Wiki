_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Hydrokinesis` - From her Aquamarine half. Bluebird Azurite can generate and control water, drawn out of whatever weapon she has in her weapon slot.
> - `Tidal Edge [2 SP]` - Her weapon lashes a jet of water at one target, even one who is Far, for 2d4 + MAG damage.
`Fairy Wings [PASSIVE]` - From her Aquamarine half. Bluebird Azurite can manifest pixie wings made of water from her gemstone. While flying, she counts as Far to anyone on the ground, and she can carry one ally of her size or smaller with her.

_``Fusion Ability:``_ 
`Steam Veil [3 SP]` - Bluebird Azurite heats her water until it boils into a thick cloud of steam around herself and one ally. Until her next turn, both of them count as Behind, and the first attack aimed at each of them has disadvantage. If her party tries to escape on its next turn, the escape can't be stopped.
_``Latent Ability:``_ 
`Cryokinesis` - Any Latent Ability either half has unlocked carries into the fusion.

``Bluebird Azurites are fusions of a Ruby and an Aquamarine, and almost all of them live among the Rogues. Most began as an Aquamarine sent out to bring a gem back and the Ruby assigned to guard her on the trip, who fell for each other along the way and never came home. Among the Rogues they do the opposite of an Aquamarine's job: they slip into imperial territory, find gems who want out, and carry them back to the rogue camps in Sector Null. The Aquamarine half knows how a hunter thinks and where she will look, and the Ruby half knows how to keep someone alive on the road. Their wings let them lift a passenger over a wall or a crowd, and their steam lets them vanish when a chase goes bad. Bluebirds are small, soft spoken, and jumpy, since every one of them knows there is a bounty out with her name on it. Inside the Gempire a Bluebird only appears through a licensed marriage, and there she works her Aquamarine half's job of retrieval, with her Ruby half as her own escort. She isn't related to the Azurites who work as court clerks. The two only share a name.``

_``Interactions:``_
``A Bluebird Azurite has good reason to stay clear of Gempire Aquamarines, since tracking down gems who have gone missing is an Aquamarine's job, and a Bluebird is half of one. The Topazes who work for Aquamarines are the ones she fears most, because a Topaz is usually the one who does the catching. Rubies on escort duty are a different story: many of them have heard how Bluebirds begin, and some look the other way. In the rogue camps, Bluebirds are the ones who bring the new arrivals in.``

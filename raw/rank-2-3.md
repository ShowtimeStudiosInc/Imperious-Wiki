# MORGANITE
**``BASE STATS``**
_``HP:``_ 30
_``ATK:``_ 5
_``DEF:``_ 5
_``SPD:``_ 5
_``INT:``_ 6
_``MAG:``_ 6
_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Bubble Generation` - Morganite can create and maintain bubbles that safely hold gems, even ones that have been bubbled for a very long time. She can use the Bubble option under <:Mercy:1568035723469127771>MERCY.
> - `Bubble [2 SP]` - Morganite bubbles one enemy who is ready to be spared, capturing them.
> - `Safekeeping [2 SP]` - Morganite bubbles a Poofed ally's gemstone. It can't be Cracked or Shattered until the bubble is popped.

_``Interactions:``_
``It only takes one Morganite to check, restore, or maintain a bubble, so most of their work is done alone. For large bubble fields, they call on each other and split the field up. Since the work is delicate, and unbubbling a gem stronger than them can go badly, Morganites are usually guarded by small groups of foot soldiers like Rubies or Eucryptites, who protect both the Morganite and the bubbles.``

# PEARL
**``BASE STATS``**
_``HP:``_ 26
_``ATK:``_ 3
_``DEF:``_ 3
_``SPD:``_ 5
_``INT:``_ 6
_``MAG:``_ 5
_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Hammerspace [PASSIVE]` - A Pearl can tuck items away into a pocket space inside her gemstone and pull them back out later. Equipping an item from her pocket space doesn't use her turn.
`Hologram Projection [1 SP]` - A Pearl makes a harmless image of light appear, for showing information or fooling the eye. One enemy who doesn't see through it attacks the image instead of its real target.
_``Latent Ability:``_ 
`Self-Duplication [2 SP]` - In rare cases, a Pearl can make a short lived copy of herself out of light, called a Holo-Pearl. It lasts 3 turns and can <:Fight:1568035371974135940>FIGHT on her turn. 
`Elemental Affinity` - Depending on her coloration, a Pearl may carry a power over one element tied to her color. Its moves and SP costs are set when it's unlocked.

``Pearls serve as the assistants of the empire. They work alongside or do tasks to help the gem they were assigned to, taking work off their hands. A Pearl is also a prize. Only high-ranked gems, or gems who have done something worthy of one, earn the right to choose a Pearl, and having one says as much about the owner as the Pearl. Pearls can change their outfits, colors, and uniform design to match their superior. Unlike most other gems who are created in kindergartens, Pearls are created in Reefs, most commonly by a Nacre.``

_``Interactions:``_
``A Pearl serves one gem only. If her superior is shattered, disgraced, or gives her up, she goes home to her Reef, where she may be presented again and placed with a new gem. Some Pearls are placed with a facility instead of a single gem.``

-# Note: changing her colors doesn't change her Elemental Affinity. That comes from her gemstone's color, not her outfit.

# SPECIALTY PEARL [SUB-TYPE OF PEARL]
**``BASE STATS``**
_``HP:``_ 28
_``ATK:``_ 3
_``DEF:``_ 4
_``SPD:``_ 5
_``INT:``_ 6
_``MAG:``_ 4
_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Hammerspace [PASSIVE]` - A Specialty Pearl can tuck items away into a pocket space inside her gemstone and pull them back out later. Equipping an item from her pocket space doesn't use her turn.
`Symbiosis [PASSIVE]` - Each Specialty Pearl is born resembling one existing gem-type, and carries a unique support ability built for gems of that type, written on her own sheet. On top of that, while she is Near an ally of the gem-type she resembles, that ally's active Signature Ability costs 1 less SP, down to a minimum of 1.
_``Latent Ability:``_ 
`Self-Duplication [2 SP]` - In rare cases, a Specialty Pearl can make a short lived copy of herself out of light. It lasts 3 turns and can <:Fight:1568035371974135940>FIGHT on her turn.
`Elemental Affinity` - Depending on her coloration, a Specialty Pearl may carry a power over one element tied to her color. Its moves and SP costs are set when it's unlocked.

``Specialty Pearls are a rare Pearl variant exclusive to Era 0 Reefs and their Nacre. Unlike standard Pearls, they cannot simply be gifted or assigned, they're won through Auction Events, making each one a genuine prize rather than a guaranteed companion. Every Specialty Pearl is born resembling the look of an existing gem-type, and carries an ability meant to complement or boost that same type's work, which makes her value at auction depend heavily on who's bidding and whether her resemblance actually lines up with their own.``

_``Interactions:``_
``A Specialty Pearl is won at auction from an Era 0 Nacre, usually by a gem of the type she resembles. Regular Pearls tend to see her as a show-off, while the gem who won her treats her as a prize. Bidding gets fierce when two gems of the same type want the same Pearl.``

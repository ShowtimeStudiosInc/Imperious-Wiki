# EUCRYPTITE
**``BASE STATS``**
_``HP:``_ 30
_``ATK:``_ 7
_``DEF:``_ 5
_``SPD:``_ 7
_``INT:``_ 4
_``MAG:``_ 2
_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Luminescence [PASSIVE]` - Eucryptite's body glows, which helps her see and be seen in almost any environment. Darkness never blinds her or dulls her senses, but she can't hide while she glows.

``Eucryptites are small but mighty grunt soldiers and scouts. They work as bodyguards and escorts, and back up other soldiers in battle. Their glow and natural versatility make them a great choice for any environment. Each one is issued a pair of daggers, one for striking and one for blocking, and is trained to guard whatever gem she's assigned to.``

_``Interactions:``_
``Eucryptites deploy in cohorts of 3 to 5, and are often paired with Ruby cohorts of the same size. The Rubies bring the brute strength, and the Eucryptites bring speed and light. Both answer to high-ranking militia like commanders and lieutenants.``

_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Thermal Manipulation [PASSIVE]` - Ruby has a small amount of heat control, enough to add a burn to her strikes. Each hit of her <:Fight:1568035371974135940>FIGHTs deals +1 Fire damage.

``Rubies are some of the most common militia gems in the empire. They serve as both foot soldiers and personal escorts to high ranking gems, often Morganites or Sapphires. ``Rubies work in groups, and are often paired with a Eucryptite cohort of the same size, with both squads answering to the same commander. Their most common posting is guarding aristocrats like Sapphires and Morganites.``

# PRASIOLITE-GOSHENITE [GEODE]
**``BASE STATS``**
_``HP:``_ 30
_``ATK:``_ 4
_``DEF:``_ 5
_``SPD:``_ 4
_``INT:``_ 5
_``MAG:``_ 5
_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Cryophrenia` - Prasgoshenite can generate and shape her own psychic energy in two ways. Compressed, it forms crystal like constructs such as barriers, projectiles, or restraints that bend light as they take shape. In its natural state, she can wrap it around beings or objects to move them, or press extra force onto a target, making them feel heavier the longer they are exposed. Her telekinesis is more precise than an Almandine's psionics, but she can lift and move far less weight.
> - `Crystal Shards [2 SP]` - Compressed psychic crystal strikes one target for 2d4 + MAG Psychic damage.
> - `Pressure [2 SP]` - One target resists with INT. If they fail, they act last in the round, and their party's abilities cost 1 more SP while they're affected, for 2 turns. It can stack up to 3 times.
_``Latent Ability:``_ 
`Technopathy [PASSIVE]` - Prasgoshenite may come to trust the gut feeling she gets when something is wrong with a machine. Most never realize it's a real ability and just think of it as intuition.

``Prasgoshenites are stationed at research facilities and high value locations, working as both the guard at the door and a helping hand in the lab. Its to be made aware there are a limited few of them these days due to their Gem-Type being discontinued since ERA 1A, partly due to Theta Gold's dislike of how many resources Geodes take to create, and also due to the new non-geode gem-types that have taken their role.``

# NACRE
**``BASE STATS``**
_``HP:``_ 30
_``ATK:``_ 3
_``DEF:``_ 5
_``SPD:``_ 5
_``INT:``_ 7
_``MAG:``_ 8
_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Life Creation` - Nacre can create Pearls, one of the only gems besides a Diamond who can make new life. This happens in the Reef, never in battle.
`Pearl Restoration` - Nacre can repair the Pearls she has created, along with Pearls other Nacre have made, on the go. As a last resort, she can also rejuvenate a Pearl she made herself.
> - `Repair [2 SP]` - Heals one Pearl, or a fusion with a Pearl half, for 2d4 + MAG. Unlike other healing, it can also mend a Pearl's cracks.

``Nacres are the only gem type capable of creating life besides the Diamonds, and gems often call them the Mother of Pearls. While they do not hold much authority compared to other high ranking figures, they have full control within the Reef, where they create Pearls and send them out to gems and places that need help with small tasks. A Nacre can rejuvenate a Pearl she made, but most see it as the hardest part of the job and only do it when nothing else can help. This gem type was created by Alpha Black Diamond, making it one of the few Legacy Gem Types that exist, and an even rarer Era 0 Legacy on top of that. Most Nacres near Homeworld are Era 0 gems, with Era 1 Nacres showing up further out, and Era 2 Nacres being the rarest of all.``

_``Interactions:``_
``Nacres work alone or in pairs, and a Reef rarely has more than two. They keep new Pearls on standby until they're chosen, and give each one etiquette training suited to the kind of higher-up she's likely to serve.``

-# Staff note: rejuvenating a player's Pearl always needs that player's agreement out of character first.

_``MAG:``_ 4
_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Gold Manipulation` - Gold can melt, move, and shape gold, drawing it from her own body.
> - `Gilded Edge [2 SP]` - Gold shapes a blade or spike of gold and strikes one target for 2d4 + MAG damage.
`Corrosion Immunity [PASSIVE]` - Gold takes no damage from Corrosive attacks and can't be affected by corrosion.

``Golds are metals that serve the gempire as both aristocrats and mercenaries. They take on combat work only when they see fit or when paid. ``A Gold doesn't lift a finger without a contract, and the Mercenary Guild is where those contracts are made and kept. Every contract is signed with the Gold's own name, something no Era 2 gem can do. To a Gold, a broken contract isn't just a lost job. It's her name on something she didn't follow through on.``

`Heavy Blow [PASSIVE]` - Wittichenite's sheer mass breaks through armor. Her <:Fight:1568035371974135940>FIGHTs ignore the target's DEF reduction.

`Wittichenites were enormous Gems produced during Omega White Diamond's original empire, with surviving specimens remembered as some of the largest individual Gems ever created. Wittichenites were primarily used for heavy labor and physically demanding assignments, with some being assigned to combat roles where their immense bodies could be put to use.`

_``Interactions:``_
``The few Wittichenites left are slow to answer to gems made after their era, and most modern gems have never stood next to one. `Prismatic Channel` - Rainbow Quartz can channel energy through any element, adapting her abilities to whatever the moment needs.
> - `Prism Bolt [2 SP]` - A bolt of energy hits one target for 2d4 + MAG damage, in any element she chooses.

`Rainbow Quartz were a highly specialized Quartz subtype from Omega White Diamond's original empire. _``Signature Ability:``_

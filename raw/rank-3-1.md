# TITANIUM
**``BASE STATS``**
_``HP:``_ 42
_``ATK:``_ 9
_``DEF:``_ 11
_``SPD:``_ 4
_``INT:``_ 3
_``MAG:``_ 2
_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Barrier Generation [2 SP] [REACTION]` - Titanium calls up a shield to block one hit aimed at herself or an ally Near her. That hit deals no damage.

``Titaniums are powerhouses of a gem, seen on the battlefield as high ranked soldiers. Being strong metals, they can take a lot of hits before poofing, and they're among the hardest gems in the empire to shatter. That's why they're sent to the front, pushing through enemy lines with a large battle hammer and a light shield. Combined with their ability to summon shields, this makes them a dangerous foe, often taking the lead in combat.``

_``Interactions:``_
``Titaniums work in teams, taking orders from the Platinums who oversee them. On the front line, they stand side by side and line up their shields into one wall against incoming fire.``


# TOPAZ
**``BASE STATS``**
_``HP:``_ 40
_``ATK:``_ 10
_``DEF:``_ 10
_``SPD:``_ 4
_``INT:``_ 3
_``MAG:``_ 3
_``ACT Bonus:``_ 
`Guard` - <:Act:1568035453842751618>ACTs that block a doorway, hold a position, or stop enemies from reaching someone are especially effective for her.
_``Latent Ability:``_ 
`Shockwave Generation` - Topaz may learn to send out a burst of force from her body. Its moves and SP costs are set when it's unlocked.

``Topazes are high ranking soldiers within the Gempire. Their duties often take place on battlefields or in high class facilities as guards, using their strength to protect other gems from threats or danger. They fight with a pugil, used for both attack and defense. Topazes often fuse with each other to get stronger, and a guard post with one tall Topaz on it may really have two.``

_``Interactions:``_
``Topazes guard facilities and fight alongside other militia, usually hand to hand. Higher-ups like Aquamarines assign them their tasks, and a Topaz often travels with an Aquamarine, Amber, or Beryl on duty.``

_``DEF:``_ 11
_``SPD:``_ 4
_``INT:``_ 3
_``MAG:``_ 3
_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Damage Transferal [2 SP]` - Black Tourmaline throws up an invisible barrier around one ally. For 2 turns, the next hit aimed at that ally lands on her instead, reduced by 2 more.
_``Latent Ability:``_ 
`Fear Immunity [PASSIVE]` - Black Tourmaline may learn to fill nearby allies with hope just by standing close. Allies Near her can't be <:Frightened:1568074311229644951>Frightened.

``Black Tourmaline works as a personal bodyguard, usually assigned to a single high value target rather than a squad. Their the wall between their charge and whatever's trying to get to them.``

_``Interactions:``_
``A Black Tourmaline is assigned to one gem and stays with her, often a Sapphire, a Diamond Pearl, or a high Authoritative. She works beside guards like Rubies and Topazes, but where they hold a door, she holds a person. Most high-ranking gems never learn her name, which is how a good Black Tourmaline prefers it.``


# DRAGON'S BREATH FIRE OPAL [SUB-TYPE OF OPAL]
**``BASE STATS``**
_``HP:``_ 32
_``ATK:``_ 9
_``DEF:``_ 6
_``SPD:``_ 4
_``INT:``_ 3
_``MAG:``_ 4
_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Fire Breath` - Fire Opal can expel a concentrated blast of flame from her mouth, strong enough to melt through metal barriers or scorch a whole line of enemies at once.
> - `Dragon's Breath [3 SP]` - A blast of flame hits every enemy who is Near for 2d4 + MAG Fire damage.
`Scaled Hide [1 SP]` - Fire Opal hardens patches of her skin into heat resistant plating. For 2 turns, every hit on her is reduced by 2 more, and her own flames can't hurt her.
_``Latent Ability:``_ 
`Heat Perception [PASSIVE]` - Fire Opal may learn to feel heat sources and flame nearby, even with her eyes closed.

``Fire Opals are shock troops built for scorched earth tactics, deployed wherever the Gempire needs a line burned through fast. They serve alongside Quartz soldiers as heavy hitters, often leading a charge rather than holding a line, since they hit far harder than they defend.``

_``Interactions:``_
``Fire Opals lead the charge for Quartz squads, with Agates and Hessonites deciding where to point them. They work well with Clinohumites and Tungstens, who can stand the heat they leave behind, and badly with anyone else caught in the blast. Most soldiers learn to stay behind a Fire Opal, never next to her.``


# TANZANITE [NON-FUSION]
**``BASE STATS``**
_``HP:``_ 42
_``ATK:``_ 9
_``DEF:``_ 11
_``SPD:``_ 4
_``INT:``_ 3
_``MAG:``_ 3
_``ACT Bonus:``_ 
`Sentry` - <:Act:1568035453842751618>ACTs that spot intruders, turn someone away, or keep order on guarded ground are especially effective for her.
_``Latent Ability:``_ 
`Crystal Blade` - Tanzanite may learn to grow her sword out of crystal from her own gemstone. Its moves and SP costs are set when it's unlocked.

``Tanzanites serve the empire as guardians for the Indigium Shrine and other shrines like it. Quartzes and Topazes can handle guard work, but shrines needed gems made for it, so Tanzanites were created as a specialty and are rarely sent anywhere else. Most are tall and stocky, with helmets that cover their eyes and large shoulder pads. They fight with large swords, used for both attack and defense.``

_``Interactions:``_
``Tanzanites work alongside each other and serve under shrine keepers like the Era 1 Indicolites. They guard parts of a shrine directly, protecting its statues, figures, and remnants. They can be assigned to war duties, but most are kept at the shrines.``

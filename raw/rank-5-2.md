# COAL
**``BASE STATS``**
_``HP:``_ 32
_``ATK:``_ 5
_``DEF:``_ 6
_``SPD:``_ 4
_``INT:``_ 3
_``MAG:``_ 1
_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Heat Generation [1 SP]` - Coal can warm parts of her body until they glow, giving off light and heat. She can't throw or shape it, only produce it from herself.

``Coals are constructive gems who aid Bismuths as assistants by different means such as providing light, encouragement or transporting building materials. They are said to resemble pearls as they were made to retrieve and follow commands, but are bulkier and stronger than them.``

_``Interactions:``_
``Coals work under Bismuths, bringing light, encouragement, and materials on almost every build. They take orders from Brown Tourmalines too, and Chrysoberyls borrow them to haul kitchen supplies. A Bismuth crew without a Coal tends to finish later and in worse moods.``


# RICHTERITE
**``BASE STATS``**
_``HP:``_ 28
_``ATK:``_ 3
_``DEF:``_ 4
_``SPD:``_ 5
_``INT:``_ 3
_``MAG:``_ 4
_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Light Solidification [1 SP]` - Richterite can form temporary tools and objects out of solid light. She can't make weapons, and her creations fade after the task is done or the scene ends.

``Richterites are able to summon items temporarily made out of spectral light to help aid with another gem's role.``

_``Interactions:``_
``Richterites are lent out to any gem who needs a tool for a moment, summoning items of light to help with that gem's work. Bismuths, Peridots, and Rhodochrosites ask for them most. Since the items fade, a Richterite usually stays until the job is finished.``


# SALT
**``BASE STATS``**
_``HP:``_ 26
_``ATK:``_ 2
_``DEF:``_ 3
_``SPD:``_ 5
_``INT:``_ 4
_``MAG:``_ 3
_``Signature Ability:``_ 
`Power Augmentation [PASSIVE]` - When Salt fuses with another gem, the fusion's Signature Ability goes up one Rating step, on top of the usual combined stats. The fusion stays the same Gem-Type.

``Salts are only ever used for their fusion boosting ability, deployed alongside groups of other gems for work or combat. Uniquely, they never change what gem type a fusion becomes, a Demantoid and a Salt will always make a bigger Demantoid, for example.``

_``Interactions:``_
``Salts travel with groups of other gems so they can fuse with one when extra power is needed. A Topaz and a Salt make a bigger Topaz, which guards rarely mind. Gems tend to forget a Salt is there until the moment they need her, and most Salts are used to that.``


# URANIUM [METAL]
**``BASE STATS``**
_``HP:``_ 8
_``ATK:``_ 5
_``DEF:``_ 3
_``SPD:``_ 3
_``INT:``_ 3
_``MAG:``_ 4
_``Signature Ability:``_
`Radiation [PASSIVE]` [Minor] [Elements] - Uranium give off radiation that sickens every Gem and Metal around them. In battle, at the start of each of their turns, every Gem and Metal Near them, Uranium included, takes 1d4 damage. Outside of battle, it affects others, but not Uranium themselves.
`Color Shift [PASSIVE]` - Uranium look gray in most light, but glow neon green in the dark or under ultraviolet light.
_``Latent Ability:``_
`Radiogoop [PASSIVE]` - Some Uranium have radioactive fluids, a neon green slime that sticks to whatever it hits. A target it sticks to takes 1d4 damage at the start of each of their turns for 2 turns, even once they're no longer Near. Many Uranium don't know they can do this.
`Nuclear Power` - Some Uranium can power machines just by touching them, for as long as they keep contact or until the battery is full. Many don't know they can do this.

``Uranium have no real job on Homeworld. Their radiation makes anyone who spends long around them sick, including themselves. Most end up as test subjects in Kandy Diamond's labs, or wired into generators to keep the power running, and neither is much of a life. Left alone, the radiation wears them down too, and most eventually crack under it.``

_``Interactions:``_
``Most gems keep their distance from a Uranium, since even a short time close to one makes them sick. Kandy Diamond's lab staff and the Silvers and Coppers who work generators beside them are the exceptions, and they rotate out often. A Malachite can pull some of the poison out of the air, so a Uranium with a Malachite friend is a lucky one.``
